"The starter home is dead."

I keep seeing that line everywhere. Here's my Greater Vancouver take: it isn't dead. It just moved into a condo.

This started as a post on my social channels. Here's that version.

## The first house is now the second or third move

The white-picket-fence first house our parents bought? Around here, that's usually the second or third move now. In the Tri-Cities, BC, today's starter home is a 1- or 2-bed condo in Coquitlam, or a townhouse in Port Coquitlam or Port Moody. And honestly? That's OK.

On the graphic: the faded house on the left is the old idea of a starter home. The bright side on the right, with the "Starter Home" sign, is what a first place looks like here now.

## Same plan, different building

The plan hasn't changed, only the building type:

1. Get in the door
2. Build equity
3. Trade up later

Waiting for the "perfect" detached house as a first purchase is how a lot of people end up renting way longer than they planned.
