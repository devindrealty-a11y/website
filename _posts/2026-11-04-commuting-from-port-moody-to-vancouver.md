Commuting from Port Moody to Vancouver comes down to three choices: the Millennium Line SkyTrain, the weekday-only West Coast Express, or driving. The right one depends on your hours, your budget and how close you live to a station, so decide before you pick a neighbourhood.

## Commuting from Port Moody to Vancouver by transit

### SkyTrain from Moody Centre or Inlet Centre

Port Moody's two SkyTrain stations, Moody Centre and Inlet Centre, are on the Millennium Line (the Evergreen extension). The line runs to VCC–Clark in East Vancouver, so to get downtown you transfer to the Expo Line, usually at Commercial–Broadway.

TransLink's published station-to-station travel times list about 27 minutes from Moody Centre to Commercial–Broadway (29 minutes from Inlet Centre), then about 10 minutes on the Expo Line to Waterfront. That's roughly 37 to 39 minutes of riding before you add the wait for your transfer. TransLink lists Millennium Line trains every 3-4 minutes at peak hours and every 6 minutes midday, and Expo Line trains to Waterfront every 2-5 minutes at peak.

The big advantage is flexibility: SkyTrain runs seven days a week, early morning to late night.

### The West Coast Express: fast, but rush hour only

The West Coast Express is TransLink's commuter train between Mission and Waterfront. It runs weekdays only: westbound to downtown in the morning, eastbound in the afternoon and evening. On the current schedule, five morning trains leave Moody Centre between 6:15 and 8:15 a.m., each arriving at Waterfront 25 minutes later. The five trains home leave Waterfront between 3:50 and 6:20 p.m.

The catch: miss the last train and you're taking SkyTrain home. It also costs more. TransLink lists a one-way adult fare between Moody Centre and Waterfront of $7.55 with Compass Stored Value, or $248.75 for an adult monthly pass. That pass also includes unlimited travel on buses, SkyTrain and SeaBus. TransLink lists West Coast Express parking at $3 a day or $60 a month.

### Fares, zones and Compass Card basics

Port Moody is in fare zone 3 and the City of Vancouver is in zone 1, according to TransLink's transit tariff. Here's what that means on SkyTrain:

- **Weekdays before 6:30 p.m.,** a trip from Port Moody to downtown is a 3-zone fare: $5.40 with Compass Stored Value or $6.70 with cash, a Compass Ticket or tap-to-pay.
- **After 6:30 p.m. on weekdays, on weekends and on statutory holidays,** every trip is a 1-zone fare ($2.85 with Stored Value).
- **Buses are always 1 zone,** and a fare is good for 90 minutes of transfers on bus, SkyTrain and SeaBus.
- **A 3-zone adult monthly pass is $211.65.**
- **Tap in and tap out on SkyTrain** so you're charged the right fare. TransLink also accepts contactless Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Interac Debit, plus Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay, for adult fares.
- **Kids 12 and under ride free.**

## Driving: Barnet Highway, Highway 7 and Highway 1

If you drive, the most direct route west is the Barnet Highway along Burrard Inlet, which links Port Moody to Hastings Street in Burnaby and on into Vancouver. It was widened to four lanes, with HOV lanes in each direction, in the 1990s. In a Tri-City News history piece, local historian Ralph Drew described it as quick on a good day and "a slog" when it's busy.

The alternatives are the Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) through Coquitlam and Burnaby, which the Province lists as meeting Highway 1 (the Trans-Canada) near Coleman Avenue, or Highway 1 itself. Port Moody council also voted in June 2026 to widen the eastbound Barnet Highway rail overpass and add a fourth eastbound lane, a $2.8 million plan that will go through the city's 2027 budget process.

## What this means for you: a newcomer's checklist

1. **Map the walk to Moody Centre or Inlet Centre** from any home you're considering if you'll ride transit daily.
2. **Match your hours to the West Coast Express.** If you regularly finish after the last train, plan around SkyTrain.
3. **Price out a month.** Compare a 3-zone SkyTrain pass with a West Coast Express pass and with parking downtown.
4. **Do a test commute at your real travel time** before you sign a lease or write an offer.
