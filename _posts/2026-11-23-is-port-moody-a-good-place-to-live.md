Is Port Moody a good place to live? For a newcomer, the draw is the inlet, the trails and SkyTrain in a smaller city in the Tri-Cities, BC, and the trade-offs are steep hills, a small purpose-built rental supply and a zone 3 commute downtown.

BC Stats puts Port Moody at 38,286 people on July 1, 2025, beside 67,736 in Port Coquitlam and 173,604 in Coquitlam. Those figures are estimates, not the census. The 2021 Census counted 33,535 people in Port Moody. I'm Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate in Port Moody, licensed since 2020. I'm a lifelong Coquitlam local and I've lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years. If you are still comparing cities, start with my [guide to relocating to Greater Vancouver](/relocating-to-greater-vancouver.html).

## What newcomers tend to like

The City of Port Moody says its trail network runs to over 60 km. The Shoreline Trail starts at the pier in Rocky Point Park and follows Burrard Inlet for about six kilometres round trip, with minimal elevation. First-week details are in my [Port Moody newcomer's guide](/blog/2026-11-02-relocating-to-port-moody-bc-newcomers-guide.html).

You also have two SkyTrain stations, Moody Centre and Inlet Centre, plus the weekday West Coast Express at Moody Centre. Walking times are in my notes on [Inlet Centre Station](/blog/2026-11-11-living-near-inlet-centre-station-port-moody.html) and [Moody Centre Station](/blog/2026-11-16-living-near-moody-centre-station-port-moody.html). A lot of shopping, work and school sits next door in Coquitlam. My [Coquitlam page](/coquitlam.html) is the comparison.

Families register with School District 43 (often called SD43). Its 2025-26 list includes Moody Elementary at 2717 St. Johns Street and Port Moody Secondary at 300 Albert Street. Catchment rules are in my [SD43 registration post](/blog/2026-11-17-sd43-school-registration-newcomers-tri-cities-bc.html).

## Hills, and the trip downtown

Port Moody is not flat once you leave the water. The City describes the Alfred Howe Greenway, linking Glenayre and College Park to the waterfront, as an intermediate to difficult trail. It rises and falls steeply through ravines, with stairs, and the round trip is 2.2 km. The official community plan says the City will keep lobbying for more transit to the north shore: Heritage Mountain, Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows and Heritage Woods. If you want a level walk to a station, start with Moody Centre or Inlet Centre. If a hill street appeals, walk the block, or ask for a video walk of it, before you decide.

TransLink's tariff puts Port Moody in zone 3 and Vancouver in zone 1. After 6:30 p.m. on weekdays, and all day on weekends and statutory holidays, a trip across the system is a one-zone fare. Times, the West Coast Express limits and driving routes are in my post on [commuting from Port Moody to Vancouver](/blog/2026-11-04-commuting-from-port-moody-to-vancouver.html). Ride it at the hour you would really travel before you sign a lease.

## Is Port Moody a good place to live if you rent?

This is where newcomers get surprised. In the October 2025 Rental Market Survey from Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC), Port Moody's primary rental market (apartments and townhouses together) had 737 units and a vacancy rate of 2.4%. CMHC rates that vacancy figure as good quality. The same table shows 4,562 units in Coquitlam and 809 in Port Coquitlam. The average rent for those Port Moody units was $2,284 and the median was $2,380, but CMHC rates both dollar figures as poor and says to use them with caution.

The sturdier dollars are for CMHC's survey zone covering the Tri-Cities, BC (Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam and Port Moody together). In that same October survey the average rent was $1,936, a one-bedroom averaged $1,708 and a two-bedroom averaged $2,175. CMHC rates the one-bedroom figure as excellent and the other two as very good. Those averages include long-time tenants. A vacant home can be offered at a different rent, and condos and suites sit outside the 737.

## What this means for you

- Choose the waterfront and a station walk, or a hill neighbourhood, before you book viewings.
- Treat the purpose-built stock as small. Reach out 4 to 6 weeks before you move, with ID, proof of income and references ready.
- Price a zone 3 commute, using the commuting post, before you pick a street.

### Is Port Moody a good place to live without a car?

It can be, near Moody Centre or Inlet Centre, if your hours fit SkyTrain or the weekday West Coast Express. The north shore is a harder match without a car. The City's plan is still asking for more transit there.

### How many purpose-built rentals are in Port Moody?

CMHC counted 737 apartments and townhouses in October 2025, with a 2.4% vacancy rate. Condos and suites are not included, and CMHC marks the $2,284 average as poor quality.

### Are the hills everywhere in Port Moody?

No. The Shoreline Trail along Burrard Inlet has minimal elevation. The Alfred Howe Greenway, and the north shore neighbourhoods in the official community plan, are the steep side.
