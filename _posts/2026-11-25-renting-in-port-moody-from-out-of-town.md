## What to have ready before you apply

I ask every newcomer for government photo ID, recent proof of income, a landlord or employer reference, and permission for a credit check. The [RCMP in British Columbia](https://rcmp.ca/en/bc/safety-tips/frauds-and-scams/rental-scams) says to be suspicious if a listing skips a credit or reference check.

## Renting in Port Moody: the rules before any money moves

A security deposit can be no more than half of the first month's rent. If pets are allowed, a pet damage deposit can be no more than half a month as well, however many pets you have. The [Province of British Columbia](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/starting-a-tenancy/deposits-fees) says a landlord cannot charge a monthly pet fee, or a fee to accept, review or process an application. Paying the security deposit establishes the tenancy, so the landlord cannot then rent the unit to someone else. If you pay and do not move in, the landlord can ask the Residential Tenancy Branch to put that deposit toward unpaid rent.

The rent you negotiate is the starting rent. For someone already living there, the [Province](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/rent-rtb/rent-increases) caps a later increase at 2.3% in 2026 and 2.2% in 2027, once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice.

On key day, walk through with the landlord and record the condition. The Province calls this a [move-in condition inspection](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/starting-a-tenancy/move-in-condition-inspection). The landlord must offer two chances to attend. If you cannot be there, name someone in writing to go for you. Skip it, and you may lose the right to your deposits.

## Seeing the unit when you are still away

The [RCMP](https://rcmp.ca/en/bc/safety-tips/frauds-and-scams/rental-scams) describes a deposit scam: the ad says the home is in high demand, you are asked to pay without a viewing, and then the messages stop. Do not send money or credit card details until you have verified what you are paying for. Arrange a walk-through and ask for picture identification. The agreement should name the landlord and give an address.

## What this means for you

The plan uses Province of British Columbia deposit rules and the RCMP warning to verify the unit before you pay.

### Can I line up a rental in Port Moody before I arrive?

Yes. Watch a live video walkthrough, and send a deposit only after you have seen the unit and checked who you are paying.

### How much can the deposit be?

No more than half of the first month's rent, and no more than half a month as a pet damage deposit if pets are allowed. There is no application fee.

### Does the 2.3% limit decide my starting rent?

You negotiate the starting rent. The 2.3% limit in 2026, and 2.2% in 2027, applies later, once every 12 months, with three full months' notice, to someone who is already a tenant.
