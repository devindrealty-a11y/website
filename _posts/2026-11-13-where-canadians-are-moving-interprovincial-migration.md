Everyone in Canada knows someone who moved to Alberta. The numbers back that up.

I shared this map on my social channels yesterday. Here's the blog version, with a bit more room for what it means if you're one of the people making the move.

## Who gained and who lost

This map shows how many people each province and territory gained or lost to the rest of Canada from April to June 2026, using Statistics Canada's latest quarterly estimates.

- **Alberta** gained the most at +5,893.
- **B.C.** was 2nd at +2,756.
- **Ontario** lost the most at -7,063, followed by Manitoba (-1,847) and Quebec (-1,233).

These are preliminary estimates, so the numbers can still be revised.

## B.C. and Alberta: almost an even trade

The part that surprised me: B.C. and Alberta are almost an even trade. 8,738 people moved from B.C. to Alberta, and 7,991 moved from Alberta to B.C. So for every 10 British Columbians heading east, about 9 Albertans are coming this way.

And our biggest source of new neighbours? Ontario, with 8,001 people moving to B.C. in just three months.
