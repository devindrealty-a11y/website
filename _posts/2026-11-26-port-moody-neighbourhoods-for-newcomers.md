Which Port Moody neighbourhood fits a newcomer? Moody Centre or Inlet Centre, including Suter Brook, if you want to walk to SkyTrain, and Heritage Mountain, Glenayre, College Park or Ioco if a bus or a car is fine. Port Moody neighbourhoods are not one kind of street. The City's 2050 plan, adopted on April 28, 2026, sets policies for fifteen of them.

I'm Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate in Port Moody, licensed since 2020. I'm a lifelong Coquitlam local and I've lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years, leasing across the Tri-Cities, BC. If you are still choosing a city, start with my [guide to relocating to Greater Vancouver](/relocating-to-greater-vancouver.html).

Moody Centre and Inlet Centre are the walk-to-station choice. The [City of Port Moody](https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/getting-around/) says Suter Brook Village is about a 5-minute walk from Inlet Centre Station, and Heritage Mountain Shoppers Village is about 11. The rest of those lists are in my [Inlet Centre](/blog/2026-11-11-living-near-inlet-centre-station-port-moody.html) and [Moody Centre](/blog/2026-11-16-living-near-moody-centre-station-port-moody.html) station guides. Most of Inlet Centre sits in a provincially set transit-oriented area.

## Heritage Mountain

[Chapter 14](https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf) groups Heritage Mountain with Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows and Heritage Woods. It calls them relatively new: detached houses and townhouses, plus a small number of apartments, and it says that forested character should stay. The same policy says the City will keep asking for more transit on the north shore.

In the timetable effective September 7, 2026, the [184](https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/september/coqp149.pdf) runs from Moody Centre Station through Inlet Centre and along Noons Creek to Panorama at Lansdowne. Bert Flinn Park is at 1300 David Avenue. Off-leash dogs are limited to the wide gravel trail from Heritage Mountain Boulevard and David Avenue, and a morning loop from 6 a.m. to 10 a.m. [School District 43](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/district-map-of-schools) lists Heritage Mountain Elementary at 125 Ravine Drive and Heritage Woods Secondary at 1300 David Avenue. Catchment follows the address, as I cover in the [SD43 registration post](/blog/2026-11-17-sd43-school-registration-newcomers-tri-cities-bc.html).

## Glenayre and College Park

The plan groups Glenayre and College Park with Harbour Heights and Seaview. Most of the housing there, and in Seaview, was built in the last 40 to 50 years, and the City says that lower-density character should generally stay. Woodland Park is the exception, from six storeys up to 12. Some streets sit beside the Suncor lands, a special study area.

The [Alfred Howe Greenway](https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/trails-and-paths/) is the link down to the water: intermediate to difficult, 2.2 km round trip, with stairs, from Westhill Park at 203 Westhill Place. The 180 runs from Lougheed Station through Burquitlam, then Glenayre, College Park and Cecile, to Moody Centre. École Glenayre Elementary is at 495 Glencoe Drive.

## Ioco

[Appendix 3](https://www.portmoody.ca/media/yjdngy5k/appendix-3-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan-appendices.pdf) calls the Ioco townsite one of the few surviving early company towns in the Lower Mainland, and a Heritage Conservation Area since 2000. Infill has to fit the scale of those low buildings. A large development waits on a full land-use plan and environmental assessment, including traffic on Ioco Road and public access to the water.

Ioco Road is on the regional Major Road Network and carries traffic to Belcarra and Anmore. The City has held nearby development back when it would add a lot of that traffic. The 181 runs between Moody Centre Station and Ioco, with some trips continuing to Anmore and Belcarra. Pleasantside Elementary is at 195 Barber Street. [Old Orchard Park](https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/parks/), at 630 Alderside Road, is the city's only swimming beach.

### Which Port Moody neighbourhood is a walk to SkyTrain?

Moody Centre and Inlet Centre, including Suter Brook. Heritage Mountain, Glenayre, College Park and Ioco mean a bus or a car, and the City is still asking for more north-shore transit.

### Can a large new project go ahead on the Ioco townsite now?

Not as a large development. The City says that waits on a comprehensive land-use plan and a full environmental assessment. Infill inside the Heritage Conservation Area has to stay compatible with the scale of the existing townsite.

### How do I find the school for a Port Moody address?

Use School District 43's school locator. Catchment is set by the address, and it can change. Schools with Port Moody addresses include Heritage Mountain Elementary, École Glenayre Elementary, Pleasantside Elementary and Heritage Woods Secondary.
