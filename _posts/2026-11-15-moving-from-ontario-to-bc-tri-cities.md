If you're moving from Ontario to BC and settling in Coquitlam, Port Moody or Port Coquitlam, a lot will feel familiar, but a few things work differently and some have deadlines that start the day you arrive. The one that surprises Ontario renters most is the deposit: the "first and last" habit doesn't carry over.

I'm Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate in Port Moody. I've been licensed since 2020, I'm a lifelong Coquitlam local, and I've lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years. Here's what I'd tell a newcomer from Ontario before moving day. For the bigger picture of the region, start with my [guide to relocating to Greater Vancouver](/relocating-to-greater-vancouver.html).

## Moving from Ontario to BC: health coverage first

- **OHIP keeps you covered for a while.** The [Government of Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ohip-coverage-outside-ontario) says that when you move permanently to another province, OHIP covers you for the same services as a temporary trip until the last day of the second full month after you leave Ontario. Prescription drugs and ambulance services aren't covered outside Ontario, so it recommends private insurance.
- **BC's MSP has a wait period.** The [Province of BC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents/eligibility-and-enrolment/how-to-enrol) says new residents may be eligible after a wait of the rest of the month you arrive plus two months. People moving from another province should keep their former plan's coverage during that wait.

So apply for MSP as soon as you've moved. Most adults finish MSP enrolment and get their BC Services Card at an ICBC driver licensing office, so you can often do it on the same visit as your licence. Once you're set up, my post on [finding a family doctor in the Tri-Cities, BC](/blog/2026-11-12-how-to-find-a-family-doctor-tri-cities-bc.html) walks through the next step.

## Your licence and car

[ICBC](https://www.icbc.com/insurance/moving-travelling/moving-BC) gives you **90 days** to switch to a BC driver's licence, and you can drive on your valid Ontario licence in the meantime. Your vehicle has a shorter clock: **30 days** after arriving to register, license and insure it in BC.

- **Bring your driving history.** ICBC will credit up to 15 years of driving experience once it has a driver's abstract showing when you were first licensed. You'll surrender your Ontario licence when you get the BC one, since BC only allows one licence ([ICBC](https://www.icbc.com/driver-licensing/moving-bc/Moving-from-within-canada)).
- **Plan for a vehicle inspection.** In most cases, an out-of-province vehicle needs a "passed" inspection report from a designated BC inspection facility before it can be registered. The exemption you may have heard about from friends in Alberta comes from the New West Partnership, and ICBC lists only Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba vehicles for it ([ICBC](https://icbc.com/vehicle-registration/buy-vehicle/registering-a-vehicle-in-B-C)). Book it early.

## Renting: what's different from Ontario

- **Deposits.** In Ontario, the [Landlord and Tenant Board](https://tribunalsontario.ca/documents/ltb/Brochures/Guide%20to%20RTA%20%28English%29.html) explains that a landlord can collect a rent deposit of up to one month's rent, and it can only be used for your last month. In [BC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/starting-a-tenancy/deposits-fees), the deposit landlords take is a security (damage) deposit, capped at half of the first month's rent, plus up to another half month as a pet damage deposit if pets are allowed. If someone in the Tri-Cities, BC asks for a full month as a security deposit, pause. My post on [BC rental deposits and scams](/blog/2026-10-31-bc-rental-deposits-and-scams.html) has more.
- **Rent increases.** Ontario's guideline for 2027 is 1.9% for most units, but the [Government of Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/residential-rent-increases) says it doesn't apply to most units first occupied after November 15, 2018. In BC, the [yearly limit](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/rent-rtb/rent-increases) is 2.3% in 2026 and 2.2% in 2027, with three full months' written notice and no more than one increase every 12 months.

## Sales tax: 13% becomes 5% + 7%

Ontario charges a 13% HST. BC charges 5% GST plus 7% PST, according to the [Canada Revenue Agency](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). That's 12% combined instead of 13%, and you'll often see two tax lines on a receipt instead of one.

## What this means for you: a checklist

1. Apply for MSP right after you arrive, and keep OHIP's end date in mind (the last day of the second full month after you leave).
2. Consider private coverage for prescriptions and ambulance trips during the gap.
3. Book an ICBC appointment for your licence and BC Services Card within 90 days.
4. Book a BC vehicle inspection, then register and insure your car within 30 days.
5. Expect a security deposit of no more than half a month's rent (plus a pet deposit if you have pets), and get everything in writing.
6. Pick your city around your commute.
