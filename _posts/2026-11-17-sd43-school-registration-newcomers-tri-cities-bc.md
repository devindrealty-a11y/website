How does SD43 school registration work for newcomers? Most families who are Canadian citizens, permanent residents, refugees or of Indigenous ancestry, and who live in BC, register directly at their neighbourhood catchment school. You'll need three documents showing your name and BC address, plus papers showing guardianship and your child's age and identity, so in practice your housing needs to be sorted first.

I'm Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate in Port Moody. I've been licensed since 2020, I'm a lifelong Coquitlam local, and I've lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years. School comes up in almost every conversation I have with newcomer families, so here's a plain-language walk-through based on School District No. 43's own pages. Always confirm details with the district or your school, because rules and dates can change.

## Who registers where with SD43

According to [SD43's registration page](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/registration-information), School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) provides public education for Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Anmore and Belcarra. Your catchment school is based on the primary home address where the parent or guardian and child live, and you can look it up with the district's [School Locator](https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43).

You go through SD43's International Education office instead if you're on a work permit, study permit, diplomatic status or visitor status, if you don't live in BC, or if you aren't the child's biological parent or legal guardian. The district's [registration guide](https://media.sd43.bc.ca/media/Default/medialib/new-student-registration-form_jan-2025.56664c1369.pdf) says not every permit qualifies for ministry-funded education. A parent's work permit, for example, must be valid for at least one year and for at least 20 hours of work a week, backed by three recent paystubs. Study permits have their own program rules, so read that section closely.

For the current school year, the guide says you can register at any time. For next school year, registration opens in January.

## SD43 school registration documents for newcomers

The registration guide lists four groups of documents:

- **Residency:** three documents with the parent or guardian's name and BC address. That's either one primary document (a long-term tenancy agreement, property purchase agreement, income tax statement or property tax statement) plus two secondary documents (such as tenant insurance, a BC Hydro bill, a BC driver's licence, BC ID or a BC Services Card), or three items like bank, credit card, phone or internet statements and pay stubs.
- **Guardianship:** the child's birth certificate listing the parents, or a court order.
- **Child's age and identity:** for example a Canadian birth certificate, passport or citizenship card, or a passport plus a document such as a permanent resident card or study permit.
- **Parent's status in Canada:** for example a Canadian passport, PR card, refugee documentation or a qualifying permit.

The guide also notes that schools at or near capacity may ask for extra residency documents. If you're missing something, the school will refer you to the School Board Office, which reviews eligibility case by case.

If English isn't your first language, the guide lists SD43's Settlement Workers in Schools (SWIS) team, who offer help in languages including Mandarin, Cantonese, Farsi, Korean, Spanish and Arabic. ## Kindergarten dates and schools near capacity

SD43's [kindergarten page](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/kindergarten-registration) says children turning five by December 31 can register in February of that year. For September 2027 entry, kindergarten registration runs February 1 to 4, 2027. Families moving in mid-year can register at any time.

Want a school other than your catchment? The [cross-catchment page](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/cross-catchment) says the 2027-2028 application window is February 5 to 10, 2027, and approval isn't automatic. Spots are filled by random draw, with siblings and in-district families first. The district lists a number of schools as at or near capacity, including Alderson, Central, Glen, Leigh, Moody, Miller Park and Smiling Creek elementary schools. So the address you rent or buy really matters.

## What this means for you: a renter's checklist

1. Run every address you're seriously considering through the School Locator before you apply.
2. Ask for a written, long-term tenancy agreement. It's one of the primary residency documents on SD43's list.
3. Set up tenant insurance and BC Hydro in your name right away, since both count as secondary documents.
4. Bring originals of birth certificates, passports and any permits or PR documents.
5. If you're on a permit, check the permit rules and contact International Education early.
6. Starting kindergarten in 2027? Mark February 1 to 4, 2027 on your calendar.
