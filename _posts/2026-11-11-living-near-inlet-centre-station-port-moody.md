Is living near Inlet Centre Station in Port Moody a good fit for a newcomer? If you want to walk to groceries, coffee, City Hall, the library and a recreation centre, and still have SkyTrain at your door, it's worth a close look. According to the City of Port Moody, Suter Brook Village is about a 5-minute walk from the station, and the Recreation Complex is about 12.

## Where is Inlet Centre Station?

TransLink lists Inlet Centre at Ioco Road and Barnet Highway, on the Millennium Line between Moody Centre and Coquitlam Central. The station's address is 2300 Barnet Hwy. It's one of two SkyTrain stops in Port Moody, so the neighbourhoods right around it (Suter Brook, Newport Village and the St. Johns Street corridor) are popular with people who want to use the car less.

If you'll be commuting into Vancouver, my post on [commuting from Port Moody to Vancouver](/blog/2026-11-04-commuting-from-port-moody-to-vancouver.html) covers SkyTrain, the West Coast Express, fares and zones, so I won't repeat that here.

## Living near Inlet Centre Station: what's within walking distance

The City of Port Moody's [Getting Around page](https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/getting-around/) lists these walking times from Inlet Centre:

- Suter Brook Village: about 5 minutes
- St. Johns Street shops: about 8 minutes
- Newport Village: about 10 minutes
- City Hall, Inlet Theatre and Port Moody Public Library: about 10 minutes
- Heritage Mountain Shoppers Village: about 11 minutes
- Recreation Complex: about 12 minutes

City Hall, the Inlet Theatre and the library all share the Civic Centre at 100 Newport Drive. The [library](https://portmoodylibrary.ca/hours-contact-and-location) says it's less than 1 km from the station, which makes it an easy first stop to get a library card and find out about local programs.

The [Port Moody Recreation Complex](https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/) at 300 Ioco Road has a weight room, two gymnasiums, two arenas, a curling centre, a walking track, a hot tub and steam room, and a childminding centre. The City lists its hours as 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. on weekdays and 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. on weekends, closed on statutory holidays.

## Buses, car share and EV charging

TransLink's [Inlet Centre station map](https://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/inlet_centre_station.pdf) shows bus bays for the 160 (Kootenay Loop and Port Coquitlam Station), the 183 (Moody Centre and Coquitlam Central stations), the 184 to Noons Creek, the N9 NightBus, and the 179 to Buntzen Lake (limited service). That means you can reach parts of Heritage Mountain and Port Coquitlam without a car, though I'd always check the current schedule for the exact stop near any home you're considering.

If you only need a car now and then, the City says Modo car share has pick-up spots on Newport Drive (Newport Village) and Capilano Road (Suter Brook Village). For electric vehicles, it lists public charging at the Civic Centre parkade and the Recreation Complex, among other spots.

## Schools for newcomer families

Port Moody is part of School District 43 (Coquitlam), which also serves Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Anmore and Belcarra. SD43's [registration page](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/registration-information) explains that your catchment school is based on your permanent home address, not on which school looks closest on a map. SD43 also says parents on a work or study permit need to contact its International Education office about registering, rather than going straight to the school. Before you sign a lease or write an offer, check the exact address in SD43's School Locator.

## What this means for you: a checklist

1. Decide what you want to walk to every day (groceries, the gym, the library, SkyTrain) and test the actual walk from the building, not just the pin on a map.
2. Check the address in the SD43 School Locator if you have kids.
3. Look up the bus routes and night service that matter to you.
4. Ask about parking, storage and EV charging in the building, since many homes here are condos and townhomes.
5. Compare it with other areas on my [Port Moody page](/port-moody.html) and [Coquitlam page](/coquitlam.html) before you commit.
