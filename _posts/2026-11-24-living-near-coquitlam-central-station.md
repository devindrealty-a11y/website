If you are weighing living near Coquitlam Central Station, it can suit a newcomer who wants a mall, a large bus exchange, SkyTrain and a weekday train downtown in the Tri-Cities, BC. [TransLink's schedule](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express) shows a 30-minute West Coast Express ride from here to Waterfront on weekday mornings.

## Where the station is

[TransLink](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain) runs the Millennium Line from VCC–Clark in Vancouver to Lafarge Lake–Douglas in Coquitlam. Coquitlam Central is on that line, east of Johnson Street at the Mariner Way overpass. The [West Coast Express page](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express) puts it at Barnet Highway and Lougheed Highway, 2920 Barnet Highway. The [Evergreen extension](https://www.canada.ca/en/transport-canada/news/2016/12/evergreen-extension-officially-open-public.html) opened on December 2, 2016.

## Living near Coquitlam Central Station: the mall and the trains

[Coquitlam Centre](https://coquitlamcentre.com/pages/contact-us/) is at 2929 Barnet Highway. The City's [City Centre Neighbourhood Plan](https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF), consolidated May 27, 2026, calls the Pinetree-Lougheed Precinct a gateway at Pinetree Way, Lougheed Highway and Barnet Highway, with shops, offices, a hotel and conference centre, and high-density homes. It also calls for crossings over Barnet Highway toward the mall and over Lougheed Highway toward Christmas Way.

[TransLink's timetable](https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt980.pdf), effective April 20, 2026, lists 2 minutes to Lincoln, 3 to Lafarge Lake–Douglas, 13 to Lougheed Town Centre and 32 to Commercial–Broadway. Downtown is a transfer, then 10 minutes on the Expo Line to Waterfront. At peak, [trains](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain) run about every 3 to 4 minutes. TransLink calls that an estimate. The line runs seven days a week.

The West Coast Express does not run all day. Five morning trains leave every 30 minutes from 6:10 to 8:10 a.m., toward downtown only. They reach Moody Centre in 5 minutes and Waterfront in 30 minutes. Five trains arrive back between 4:19 p.m. and 6:49 p.m. The [June 8, 2026 timetable](https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt997.pdf) has no weekend or statutory-holiday service (public holidays the province sets).

The [April 2023 map](https://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/wce_station_maps/coquitlam_central_station.pdf) shows the R3 RapidBus, a faster bus toward Haney Place, the 160, the 183, the 701 and the N9 late-night bus downtown. Routes change, so check the trip planner.

[Town Centre Park](https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Town-Centre-Park-84), at 1299 Pinetree Way, is reached from Lafarge Lake–Douglas, 3 minutes east. The [City Centre library](https://www.coqlibrary.ca/about-us/hours-and-locations) is at 1169 Pinetree Way.

## Fares, the park-and-ride and schools

Coquitlam is fare zone 3 and Vancouver is zone 1 in TransLink's [transit tariff](https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/about-translink/governance-and-board/bylaws/transit-tariff-bylaw-162-2026.pdf), effective July 1, 2026. Before 6:30 p.m. on a weekday, SkyTrain downtown is a 3-zone fare: $5.40 adult stored value on a Compass Card (the reloadable fare card), or $6.70 cash, ticket or bank-card tap, on the [fare page](https://www.translink.ca/transit-fares/pricing-and-fare-zones). After 6:30 p.m. weekdays, and all day weekends and statutory holidays, it is 1 zone at $2.85 stored value. Buses are always 1 zone. A regular fare lasts 90 minutes. The West Coast Express lasts 120 minutes and has its own price.

[West Coast Express fares](https://www.translink.ca/transit-fares/west-coast-express-fares) group this station with Moody Centre and Port Coquitlam as 3 zones to or from Waterfront: $7.55 adult stored value, or $9.05 by ticket or tap. An adult monthly pass is $248.75.

The [park-and-ride](https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/driving/park-and-ride) at 2920 Barnet Highway lists 934 spaces, including 15 electric-vehicle chargers run by BC Hydro, seven days from 4 a.m. to 2 a.m., at $3 a day or $60 a month. Overnight parking is not allowed, and construction can cut the stall count.

[School District 43 (Coquitlam)](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/registration-information) sets the catchment from the home address. Use the [School Locator](https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43). A parent on a work or study permit contacts International Education instead.

## What this means for you: a checklist

1. Video the walk to the station, the buses and the mall.
2. If your hours miss those trains, or you work weekends, plan on SkyTrain.
3. Check today's buses, including the N9 if you are out late.
4. Ask about a parking stall. The park-and-ride closes overnight.
5. Run the address through the School Locator if you have kids.
6. Compare streets on my [Coquitlam page](/coquitlam.html), and see my [relocating to Greater Vancouver page](/relocating-to-greater-vancouver.html) for the wider move.

### How long is the ride from Coquitlam Central to Waterfront?

Thirty minutes on the weekday West Coast Express, with morning trains from 6:10 to 8:10 a.m. SkyTrain is 32 minutes to Commercial–Broadway, then a transfer and 10 minutes to Waterfront.

### What does a weekday trip downtown cost?

Before 6:30 p.m., adult stored value is $5.40 on SkyTrain and $7.55 on the West Coast Express. After 6:30 p.m., and all weekend, SkyTrain is a 1-zone fare. The commuter train does not run then.
