## The seven items from the caption

**1. Medical Services Plan (MSP).** Enrol early. The [Province of B.C.](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/health-drug-coverage/msp/bc-residents/eligibility-and-enrolment/how-to-enrol/coverage-wait-period) explains the coverage wait period for new residents. If you're moving from another province, keep your old plan during the wait.

**2. ICBC.** [ICBC](https://www.icbc.com/insurance/moving-travelling/moving-BC) sets the deadlines for switching your driver's licence and registering a vehicle when you move to B.C. Book the appointment before the clock runs out.

**3. BC Hydro.** Open your [electricity account](https://app.bchydro.com/accounts-billing/moving-electrical-connections/new-customer.html) so the power is in your name on move-in day. If the home uses natural gas, set up FortisBC too, and book internet while you're at it.

**4. Tenant insurance.** Many landlords require it. Get a policy that fits your stuff and your lease before you unpack everything.

**5. Compass Card.** [TransLink](https://www.translink.ca/transit-fares/compass-card) uses a reloadable Compass Card for SkyTrain, buses and SeaBus. In the Tri-Cities, BC, the Millennium Line (Evergreen Extension) is the everyday link into Burnaby and Vancouver.

**6. School.** Families in Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Anmore and Belcarra register with [School District No. 43 (Coquitlam)](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/registration-information). Start that paperwork early if kids need a seat for the current school year.

**7. A family doctor.** Once you have your Personal Health Number, use the [Health Connect Registry](https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/health-connect-registry) or call 8-1-1. Then, as the TikTok says, buy a real rain jacket and go outside.
