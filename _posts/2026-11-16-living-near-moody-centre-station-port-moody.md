Is living near Moody Centre Station in Port Moody a good fit for a newcomer? If you'd like to walk to the waterfront, local shops and a community centre, and have both SkyTrain and the West Coast Express at one station, it's worth a look. According to the City of Port Moody, Rocky Point Park is about a 10-minute walk from the station and the St. Johns Street shops about 8.

I'm Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate in Port Moody. I've been licensed since 2020, I'm a lifelong Coquitlam local, and I've lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years. Last week I wrote about [living near Inlet Centre Station](/blog/2026-11-11-living-near-inlet-centre-station-port-moody.html), Port Moody's other SkyTrain stop. This post covers the older, waterfront end of the city.

## Where is Moody Centre Station?

TransLink lists Moody Centre Station at 65 Williams Street, on the south side of the CP tracks near Spring Street. It's a Millennium Line SkyTrain station and also a West Coast Express stop, so you have two rail options from one platform area.

On weekdays, TransLink's [West Coast Express schedule](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express) shows five westbound morning trains leaving Moody Centre from 6:15 to 8:15 a.m., each arriving at Waterfront 25 minutes later. Five eastbound trains reach Moody Centre between 4:14 and 6:44 p.m. The West Coast Express runs toward downtown only in the morning and back only in the afternoon and evening, so outside those times you'd use SkyTrain. My post on [commuting from Port Moody to Vancouver](/blog/2026-11-04-commuting-from-port-moody-to-vancouver.html) compares the options, fares and zones in more detail.

## Living near Moody Centre Station: what's within walking distance

The City of Port Moody's [Getting Around page](https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/discover-port-moody/getting-around/) lists these walking times from Moody Centre Station:

- St. Johns Street shops: about 8 minutes
- Rocky Point Park: about 10 minutes
- Clarke Street shops: about 12 minutes
- Queen Street Plaza: about 13 minutes
- Kyle Centre: about 13 minutes
- Shoreline Trail: about 16 minutes

[Rocky Point Park](https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/parks/rocky-point-park/) at 2800 Murray Street has a recreational pier, playground, outdoor pool, skateboard park, bike trials park and boat launch, plus kayak and paddleboard rentals. The City says its spray park usually opens for the May long weekend and runs until September, 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., weather permitting.

[Kyle Centre](https://www.portmoody.ca/parks-recreation-and-environment/recreation/recreation-facilities-and-locations/) at 125 Kyle Street has activity and craft rooms, a lounge and a hall for recreation programs and community activities. The City lists it as open Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

## Buses, car share and EV charging

TransLink's [Moody Centre station map](https://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/moody_centre_station.pdf) (dated April 2018) shows bus bays for the 160, 180, 181 Ioco, 182 Belcarra, 183 and 184 Noons Creek. Routes and schedules change, so check TransLink's current trip planner for the stop nearest any home you're considering.

The City lists a Modo car share pick-up spot on Spring Street at Moody Centre Station, and public EV charging at Kyle Centre and Rocky Point Park, among other locations.

## Growth around the station

The area around Moody Centre is changing. The City's [Transit Oriented Areas page](https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/transit-oriented-areas/) explains that under provincial Bill 47, residential land within 800 metres of Moody Centre or Inlet Centre Station can allow minimum heights of up to 20 storeys within 200 metres, up to 12 storeys from 201 to 400 metres, and up to 8 storeys from 401 to 800 metres. Port Moody also removed minimum residential off-street parking requirements in these areas. For a newcomer, that can mean new buildings and construction nearby over the next few years, and some newer buildings may have fewer parking stalls, so ask early if you need one.

## What this means for you: a checklist

1. Walk (or have someone video) the actual route from the building to the station, the shops and Rocky Point Park.
2. If you'll commute downtown, compare West Coast Express times with your work hours, and have a SkyTrain backup.
3. Check current bus routes from the nearest stop, not an old map.
4. Ask whether a parking stall, storage locker and EV charging come with the unit.
5. Ask about any nearby construction or development applications that could affect noise or views.
6. If you have kids, confirm the catchment school for the exact address with School District 43.
