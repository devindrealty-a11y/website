Is living near Lafarge Lake–Douglas Station a good fit for a newcomer? It can be, if you want Town Centre Park, a pool and City Hall beside the Coquitlam end of the Millennium Line, and the SkyTrain time into Vancouver fits your day. [TransLink's timetable](https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt980.pdf), effective April 20, 2026, lists 35 minutes on the train to Commercial–Broadway, before any transfer toward downtown.

## Where the station is

[TransLink](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain) runs the Millennium Line between VCC–Clark and Lafarge Lake–Douglas, so this station is the terminus in Coquitlam. [Transport Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/transport-canada/news/2016/12/evergreen-extension-officially-open-public.html) says the Evergreen extension opened on December 2, 2016. The station is at Pinetree Way and Guildford Way. The [April 2018 station map](https://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/skytrain_station_maps/lafarge_lake_douglas_station.pdf) lists 1201 Pinetree Way.

Coquitlam's [City Centre Neighbourhood Plan](https://coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17458/Official-Community-Plan---Schedule-J-PDF), consolidated April 27, 2026, calls this corner the Four Corners Precinct: mostly City-owned land holding City Hall, part of Town Centre Park, the aquatic complex, Evergreen Cultural Centre and a temporary park-and-ride.

## Living near Lafarge Lake–Douglas Station: the park and civic corner

[Town Centre Park](https://www.coquitlam.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Town-Centre-Park-84) at 1299 Pinetree Way is reached by transit from the station. That City page also lists City Hall at 3000 Guildford Way, open weekdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The park is a hundred acres around Lafarge Lake. The lake is stocked with rainbow trout from March through November.

The [City Centre Aquatic Complex](https://www.coquitlam.ca/351/Swimming-Aquatics) at 1210 Pinetree Way is open 5:30 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. Monday to Saturday and 7 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. Sunday. Poirier Sport & Leisure Complex, at 633 Poirier Street, is a different pool.

[Evergreen Arts](https://evergreenarts.ca/about/) says its centre at 1205 Pinetree Way overlooks Lafarge Lake and is just steps from the lake and this SkyTrain line. [Douglas College](https://www.douglascollege.ca/about-douglas/campus-information/addresses-and-maps) puts the Coquitlam campus at 1250 Pinetree Way, 1 kilometre north of Coquitlam Centre, and its [July 15, 2026 FAQ](https://www.douglascollege.ca/sites/default/files/docs/student-life/new_students_faq.pdf) says the campus is about a 6-minute walk from the station. The [City Centre library](https://www.coqlibrary.ca/about-us/hours-and-locations) at 1169 Pinetree Way is open 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday to Thursday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Friday, and 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Saturday and Sunday.

[Coquitlam Centre](https://coquitlamcentre.com/pages/contact-us/) is at 2929 Barnet Highway. Use the trip planner, or the 1-minute ride to Lincoln, before you count the mall as a walk from this door.

## The ride, the buses and the bike parkade

The timetable is train time only: 1 minute to Lincoln, 16 to Lougheed Town Centre and 35 to Commercial–Broadway. Add its 10 minutes from Commercial–Broadway to Waterfront, plus the transfer wait, to reach downtown. Peak trains run about every 3 to 4 minutes. TransLink calls that frequency an estimate.

The 2018 map shows the 183, 186, 188, 191, the 160 to Kootenay Loop and the N9 NightBus. The [bike parkade](https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/bike-and-ride-on-transit/bike-parking) is open to enrolled Compass Card users for $1 a day, up to $8 a month, 24 hours a day.

Within 800 metres, [transit-oriented area rules](https://www.coquitlam.ca/1360/Transit-Oriented-Areas) stop the City from requiring minimum parking and require it to allow provincial height minimums. A [July 2024 bulletin](https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/13177) puts those at up to 20 storeys within 200 metres, 12 from 200 to 400 metres, and 8 from 400 to 800 metres. The temporary park-and-ride is to move within 400 metres of the station.

[SD43](https://www.sd43.bc.ca/registration-information) sets the catchment from the home address. Check it in the [School Locator](https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43). [liv.rent](https://liv.rent/blog/rent-reports/november-2026-metro-vancouver-rent-report/) put Coquitlam's November 2026 average asking rent for a newly listed unfurnished one-bedroom at $2,057. That is a city-wide ad figure, so price the listing in front of you.

## What this means for you: a checklist

1. Video the walk to the station, the park, the pool and the library.
2. Time a real downtown trip in the trip planner.
3. Check current buses, including the N9 if you travel late.
4. Ask if a stall is included, and what is happening with the park-and-ride and nearby construction.
5. Run the exact address through the SD43 School Locator if you have kids.
6. Compare areas on my [Coquitlam page](/coquitlam.html). On a work or study permit, contact SD43 International Education before you register.
