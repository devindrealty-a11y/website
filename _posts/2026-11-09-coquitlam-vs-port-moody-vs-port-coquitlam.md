## Port Moody: fleece, trails and inlet air

Port Moody is the "fleece and a golden retriever" energy in that video. Rocky Point Park, the Shoreline Trail and two Millennium Line stations ([Inlet Centre and Moody Centre](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/skytrain/)) make it easy to live close to water and transit. Many newcomers picture weekend walks, a dog, and a shorter SkyTrain hop into Burnaby or Vancouver. If that sounds like you, read my [Port Moody newcomers guide](/blog/2026-11-02-relocating-to-port-moody-bc-newcomers-guide.html) and my [Port Moody area page](/port-moody.html).

## Coquitlam: the 38th-floor option (and a few others)

Coquitlam is bigger, so the personality split is wider. City Centre and Burquitlam lean high-rise and SkyTrain-close: Lincoln, Coquitlam Central, Lafarge Lake–Douglas and Burquitlam stations put malls, [Town Centre Park / Lafarge Lake](https://www.coquitlam.ca/facilities/facility/details/Town-Centre-Park-84) and Douglas College within reach. Burke Mountain and Westwood Plateau feel more "space and a driveway." If you're torn between a tower and a townhome hillside, my [Coquitlam first-30-days checklist](/blog/2026-11-06-relocating-to-coquitlam-bc-first-30-days.html) and [Coquitlam page](/coquitlam.html) help you match commute and budget. For the station-side condo lane, see the [Burquitlam neighbourhood guide](/burquitlam-neighbourhood-guide.html).

## Port Coquitlam: truck, trail and neighbours who wave

Port Coquitlam (PoCo) is the "truck and neighbours who know your name" side of the TikTok. Single-family streets, the [Traboulay PoCo Trail](https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/recreation-parks/parks-trails/traboulay-poco-trail), and a weekday [West Coast Express](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express/) stop shape a different day-to-day than a tower next to SkyTrain. If your job is nine-to-five downtown on weekdays, test that train schedule before you sign. Details are in my [Port Coquitlam newcomers guide](/blog/2026-11-08-relocating-to-port-coquitlam-bc-newcomers.html) and [Port Coquitlam page](/port-coquitlam.html).

## Can't decide? Start with commute, pets and building type

I help people choose by three practical filters, not vibes alone:

1. **Commute.** SkyTrain-heavy days point toward Port Moody or Coquitlam station neighbourhoods. West Coast Express weekdays point toward PoCo, Moody Centre or Coquitlam Central, with a backup plan for evenings and weekends.
2. **Pets and parking.** A golden retriever plus underground parking is a different search than a truck and a driveway. Say that up front.
3. **Building type.** High-rise strata near transit is a different lifestyle (and strata fee reality) than a house with neighbours who borrow the ladder.
