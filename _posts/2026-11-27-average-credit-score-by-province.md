## A caveat up front

Equifax and TransUnion don’t publish official average scores by province. These are 2026 estimates, based on [Borrowell’s Equifax Risk Score data](https://borrowell.com/blog/highest-canadian-credit-score-study) from more than 4 million members in Canada’s largest cities. [Loans Canada](https://loanscanada.ca/credit/average-credit-score-by-province/) published the same unweighted city means on June 19, 2026, and [Credit Library](https://creditlibrary.ca/average-credit-score-canada/) repeated that city table on October 3, 2026.

N.B., P.E.I., N.L. and the territories aren’t in that city list, so they’re marked as no data. I didn’t invent numbers for them.

The scores are Equifax Risk Scores, on a scale of 300 to 900. They are not FICO scores. Borrowell members signed up for a free credit-monitoring service, so this is a large sample, not a census of every credit-active Canadian.

## Where the provinces land

The Canadian average is 679. That national figure is Borrowell’s. It is not a mean of the provincial estimates.

- Quebec leads at about 707.
- Ontario is about 685.
- B.C. sits near 684, a little above the national average. That estimate is Vancouver at 704 and Surrey at 664 only.
- Nova Scotia is 669, from Halifax only.
- Saskatchewan and Alberta are both about 659. Saskatchewan is Saskatoon only. Alberta is Calgary at 671 and Edmonton at 646.
- Manitoba is the lowest of the provinces with data, at about 657, from Winnipeg only.

The city means are unweighted. A population-weighted mean would look different, and smaller cities and rural areas can run differently from the cities on this list.

## What this means if you’re buying in Greater Vancouver

A higher score can mean a better mortgage rate and an easier time qualifying. Payment history is the biggest factor, typically about 35% of the score. That is general scoring guidance, not one lender’s cutoff, and not a rate you are offered.

If you’re buying here, don’t paste 684 onto a street in the Tri-Cities, BC. The B.C. figure is Vancouver and Surrey only. It is not your score.
