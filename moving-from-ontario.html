FAQ

Questions about moving from Ontario

Can I lease before I arrive?

Yes. You don't have to fly in for every viewing. I can search, walk homes with you on live video, and go through the lease so you can sign a written tenancy agreement before you land. Most Greater Vancouver rentals are listed only a few weeks before they're available, so the active search usually starts about 4 to 6 weeks before your move-in date.

How do deposits work in B.C.?

In B.C., a security deposit can be at most half of one month's rent. If pets are allowed, a pet damage deposit can be at most another half month, no matter how many pets you have. Together the two deposits can equal one month's rent. Landlords in B.C. can't require "first and last month's rent" the way some other provinces do, and they can't charge a fee just to accept or process an application. Confirm the current rules on the Province of B.C. deposit page. This is general information, not legal advice.

What does it cost?

The tenant-side search is $1,500 total + GST, paid by you, the tenant, in two parts: $750 at the beginning of the engagement (non-refundable) and $750 once you successfully secure a rental. The free renter's guide does not require this service. More on the fee is in What it costs.
