# Creative briefs

Four stills and one video treatment. Build the files outside this repo (Canva, Meta’s video tools, or any editor). Do not commit finished ads to the site, and do not replace `assets/video/greater-vancouver-relocation-ad.mp4`.

No MLS photos, no REALTOR.ca watermarks, no listing addresses, no prices pulled from the feed. Those photos belong to the listing and the DDF rules are stricter than a social post. Use Devin’s own headshot, the Axford logo, and type.

Logo files already in the repo:

## Still 1 — Renter, “before you arrive” (R1 and R3)

**Use with:** R1 and R3.

**Picture:** Devin’s headshot, left or upper third, on `#0d1b2a`. A thin `#3474b6` rule under the eyebrow.

**On-image text, and nothing else:**

- Eyebrow: `MOVING TO GREATER VANCOUVER`
- Line (R1): `Line up your rental before you arrive.`
- Line (R3): `See the home on a live video first.`

**Do not show:** keys in someone’s hand as a “we found it” promise, a stock family, a “SOLD” banner, or a map pin on a specific building.

## Still 2 — Renter, fee (R2 and R4)

**Use with:** R2 and R4.

**Picture:** No headshot. Type on `#e8f0f9` with ink text, so it looks like the site’s paper sections and reads as a price, not a lifestyle ad.

**On-image text (R2):**

- Eyebrow: `TENANT-SIDE RENTAL HELP`
- Price: `$1,500 + GST`
- Sub: `Paid in two parts`
- Two lines: `$750 at the start` and `$750 when you secure a rental`
- Footer: `Devin Desaulniers · Axford Real Estate`

The first $750 is non-refundable on the site. The primary text already says that. If the image can fit one more line without becoming a paragraph, add `First $750 is non-refundable`. If it cannot, leave it in the primary text only. Do not shorten the fee to “$1,500” with the GST dropped.

**On-image text (R4):** same layout, replace the two fee lines with `Start about 4-6 weeks before your move` and keep `$1,500 + GST` and `Paid in two parts`.

## Video — homepage relocation film

The site already has a 32-second film at `assets/video/greater-vancouver-relocation-ad.mp4` (1280 × 720, used on the home page). It can be a fifth creative on the **renter** ad set only, and only after Devin watches it and confirms every spoken and on-screen line is in the “allowed claims” list in [copy.md](copy.md).

If any line promises a result, names a price that is not $1,500 + GST, or shows an MLS photo, do not run that file. Cut a new export. Leave the file in `assets/video/` as it is.

**Feed:** the 1280 × 720 file can run in Feed. Captions burned in, because most people watch with sound off. End card for the last 3 seconds: `Devin Desaulniers · Axford Real Estate` and `devindesaulniers.ca`.

**Stories / Reels:** a separate 1080 × 1920 export. Centre the existing frame in the safe area, pad with `#0d1b2a`, same end card. Do not stretch the 16:9 picture to fill 9:16.

**Primary text for the video ad:** use R1. **URL:** the R1 relocating-guide URL. **Button:** Learn more.

This video does not get its own budget. It is one more ad inside the renter ad set. If it spends and the stills do not, that is fine. If its cost per landing-page view is more than double the stills after $40 of its own spend, turn that one ad off.
