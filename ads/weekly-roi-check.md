# Weekly ROI check

One sitting, every Monday, about 20 minutes. The sheet below is the record. Copy it into a note Devin keeps (a doc or a spreadsheet). It does not have to live in this repo after launch, because it will contain client details.

**Source of truth:** the spend screens in Meta and Google, plus the invoices he has sent, plus the commission he has received. Ads Manager “results” are landing-page views until the pixel exists. Do not compute the ratio from those results.

## When

| Monday | What the check is |
| --- | --- |
| 2 November 2026 | Launch check only. Confirm the three campaigns are approved, the spending limits match November, and a form email would reach devin@axfordrealestate.ca. Do not judge the ratio. |
| 9, 16, 23, 30 November 2026 | Weekly check. 30 November is also the decision to extend into December or stop. |
| 7, 14, 21, 28 December 2026 | Weekly check. 28 December is the January decision (or stop). |
| 4, 11, 18, 25 January 2027 | Weekly check, and only if the campaigns are still on. |
| The Monday after the last ad dollar, and again 60 days after that dollar | Close the books. Final ratio. |

If spend hits $1,000 on a Thursday, do the stop that day. Do not wait for Monday to pause.

## Qualified leads

A **qualified renter** is a person who:

- is moving from outside Greater Vancouver (or from well outside the Tri-Cities, with a real commute change), and
- has a move-in window, and
- has heard the fee is $1,500 + GST in two parts and has not refused it outright

The usual evidence is a **New relocation rental intake** email, or a call that covers the same facts. A **New rental inquiry** about one listing is not a qualified renter unless the conversation reaches that definition.

A **qualified buyer** is a person who:

- is relocating to Greater Vancouver, and
- gave an area, a price range, and a timeline inside 12 months

The usual evidence is a **New buyer listings request** email or a call that covers the same facts. “Just curious” with no timing is not qualified.

## Recognized return this week

Add only:

- tenant fee invoices sent this week, before GST (a $750 retainer counts as $750; the second $750 counts in the week it is invoiced)
- buyer commission that hit Devin’s side of the split this week, on a file he has marked as coming from this test

Write the buyer dollar as he received it. Do not estimate.

## The Monday steps

1. **Spend.** In each campaign, note spend since 1 November (cumulative) and spend since the previous Monday.
2. **Cap.** Add Meta + Google cumulative. If that sum is **$1,000 or more**, pause all three campaigns before doing anything else.
3. **Leads.** From the inbox, count this week’s emails with subjects “New relocation rental intake”, “New buyer listings request”, and “New rental inquiry”. Ask on any call with no form. Mark Meta, Google, or unknown.
4. **Qualified.** Apply the definitions above. Drop test messages.
5. **Money.** Add invoices and received commission for the week. Update the cumulative return.
6. **Ratio.** Cumulative return ÷ cumulative spend. Blank if spend is $0.
7. **Search terms.** Google only: add negatives for junk queries. Confirm no broad-match keyword appeared on its own.
8. **Creative.** In the renter ad set, pause any single ad whose cost per landing-page view is more than double the best ad, after that ad has spent at least $40. Leave at least two renter ads on.
9. **Decision.** Use the rules below. Write one sentence: continue, shift, or stop.
10. **Reply speed.** If any lead from the week sat more than one business day with no reply, fix that before raising a budget. Slow replies waste the only spend that is working.

## Decisions

| What you see | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Cumulative spend ≥ $1,000 | Pause everything. |
| End of November, at least one qualified renter or one qualified buyer, spend under the cap | Raise the spending limits by one more month at the same split ($180 / $60 / $60) and extend the end date. |
| End of November, zero qualified leads | Stop. Do not start a second month. |
| Renter spend has passed $300 and there are still zero qualified renters | Pause the renter campaign. Leave buyers and Google on only if they have a qualified lead of their own; otherwise stop those too. |
| Cumulative spend ≥ $600 and invoiced return is $0 | Stop the whole test. |
| Cumulative spend ≥ $600 and return is under $1,200, but a second placement is underway (retainer invoiced, or a buyer agreement he expects to close) and the rest fits under $1,000 | Continue, without raising the monthly pace. |
| Cumulative spend ≥ $600 and return is under $1,200, and nothing is underway | Stop. |
| A renter ad is clearly more expensive per landing-page view after $40 | Pause that ad. Do not pause the whole campaign for one weak ad. |
| Google has spent $80 with zero inquiries and zero calls | Pause the Google campaign. Keep Meta if Meta is inside its own rules. |
| Montreal is eating the renter budget with almost no landing-page views (week of 9 or 16 November) | Remove Montreal from both Meta ad sets. |
| Ratio is 2.0 or better and the cap is not hit | Keep the same monthly pace. Do not jump to $500 just because one retainer landed. One fee can make a small spend look finished; the next $200 still has to obey the cap. |

Shifting money from a campaign that failed its gate onto another campaign is allowed only inside the **same** monthly limit. Example: Google is paused at $40 spent of its $60, so $20 can move to the renter spending limit. The November total stays $300.

**Ratio** = (tenant fees invoiced + buyer commission received) ÷ total spend.

Worked example, so the column order is obvious: $180 renter spend, $40 buyer spend, $20 Google, one $750 retainer, no buyer commission. Total spend $240. Return $750. Ratio 3.13. That week continues. It does not authorize a jump to $500.

## Close the books

Sixty days after the last ad dollar:

- Final spend (should be ≤ $1,000)
- Final tenant fees invoiced
- Final buyer commission received
- Ratio
- One line on what to repeat: which audience, which of R1–R4 or B1–B4, whether Google earned a second test

If the ratio is under 2.0, the next test needs a new written plan. Turning the campaigns back on is not the default.
