November 15 — pick the winner

  • Winner = lowest cost per qualified inquiry (spend on that ad ÷ inquiries that person tied to that picture). A qualified inquiry is a real relocation, a workable budget and date, and a willingness to hear the fee.
  • Ask: “Was it the video, or a photo — the skyline, the SkyTrain, or the trees and lake?” If they are not sure, do not guess an ad.
  • Zero qualified inquiries on all four: no winner, no jump to $200. Keep the cheapest click at $100 / month through November 30, then pause unless the calls gave a written reason to continue.
  • Prove-out, later: a retainer in the bank, return at least 2.0, and the inquiries still match that definition. Then a higher budget can be discussed. This test does not raise it.
