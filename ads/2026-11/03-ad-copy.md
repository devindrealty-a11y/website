# Ad copy

Four variants per audience. Voice matches the site: plain, first person, fee visible, no promise that a home will be found.

Paste the primary text, headline, and description into those Meta fields. The picture carries its own short headline; that overlay is specified in [04-creative-briefs.md](04-creative-briefs.md). Button: **Learn more**.

Every renter ad includes, in the first lines, the fee: **$1,500 + GST, paid by the tenant.** Do not shorten this to “$750.” The second half is due only when a rental is secured, and the page explains the split. The ad price is the full service.

Brokerage line, in the description and on the image: **Devin Desaulniers, REALTOR® · Axford Real Estate**.

Avoid: “guaranteed,” “best realtor,” “cheap rent,” “limited spots,” review quotes, and any line that excludes people by family status, age, or origin. “Individuals, couples, and families” is fine because it does not shut anyone out. The site already uses that phrasing.

### How to read the four

The test compares the video with the stills. Each still has its own headline so the words match the picture. Treat the winner as the whole ad (picture + this copy). After November 14, new sentences get tried on the winning picture, one at a time, inside the $100–$200 month. Do not launch a fifth ad during the $100.

## Audience B — relocating buyers (parked)

No spend in the $100. When a buyer flight happens, it replaces renter spend inside $100–$200 a month. It does not run beside it.

These ads do not quote $1,500. That fee is the tenant service. On the first call, explain how buyer representation is paid. Do not invent a number in the ad.

Destination: `buyers.html#listings`. URLs are in the landing-page doc.

### B1 — Buying from out of province (`utm_content=buyer-remote`)

**Primary text**

Moving to Greater Vancouver to buy? I help out-of-province buyers shortlist areas, watch listings, and plan offers before they fly in.

I’m Devin Desaulniers, a REALTOR® with Axford Real Estate in Port Moody. Send the budget, the timing, and where you need to be for work.

**Headline:** Moving here, and buying?

**Description:** Devin Desaulniers, REALTOR® · Axford Real Estate

Tell me your range and your timing. I’ll send listings that match, and I’ll say when an area does not.

**Headline:** Buying in the Tri-Cities?

**Description:** Devin Desaulniers, REALTOR® · Axford Real Estate

### B3 — New job (`utm_content=buyer-newjob`)

Devin Desaulniers, REALTOR®, Axford Real Estate, Port Moody.

### B4 — Rent first, then buy (`utm_content=buyer-rent-first`)

Use this only with the **relocating** URL, not `buyers.html`. The sentence offers the tenant service, and that page states the fee and the “rent now, buy later” path.

**Primary text**

Rent first, learn Greater Vancouver, buy when you are ready. I can help with the rental now and stay your local contact afterward.

Tenant Relocation Service: $1,500 + GST, paid by the tenant. I’m Devin Desaulniers, REALTOR® with Axford Real Estate.

**Headline:** Rent first. Buy when it fits.

**Description:** Devin Desaulniers, REALTOR® · Axford Real Estate

B4 is a renter offer with a buyer horizon. It is not one of the four November ads. It is the spare hook if, later, calls keep saying “we might buy next year.”

## Audience C — Google Search (optional, later)

Responsive search ads. Pin nothing. Do not turn these on in November.

Final URL for the relocation group: the renter landing URL with `utm_source=google`, `utm_medium=cpc`, `utm_campaign=gv-search-later`, `utm_content=relocation-realtor`.

Final URL for the Coquitlam rentals group: `https://devindesaulniers.ca/rentals.html?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=gv-search-later&utm_content=coquitlam-rentals`

The rentals page is a listing search. Its inquiry is about a specific home. Copy for that group should offer the search, not the $1,500 service. Mixing the fee into a “Coquitlam rentals” ad sends the wrong person to the wrong promise.
