## December onward — until it proves out

Each Monday, five minutes, same sheet.

Stay at $100–$200 a month on the winner. “Proved out” means all three:

1. At least one Tenant Relocation Service retainer has been collected.
2. **Collected fees ÷ total ad spend is at least 2.0.**
3. The inquiries still match the definition in the tracking plan (real relocation, workable budget and date, willing to hear the fee).

Until those are true, do not raise the month above $200 and do not add a second audience with new money. Scaling is a later decision, after this test.

## Stop if the fees never arrive

At 5% GST the full fee is **$1,575** and the retainer is **$787.50**. Confirm GST with Axford; the ads say “+ GST”, not a tax-included total.

One full fee covers **$787** of spend at a 2:1 return ($1,575 ÷ 2). If cumulative spend reaches **$787** and collected fees are still **$0**, pause every ad. Another month cannot hit 2:1 on a single future fee once spend has passed that line.

The November 1–14 flight is allowed to finish at $100 even with zero fees. The $787 stop applies to the prove-out phase, and immediately if spend ever crosses $787 with nothing collected.
