# Creative briefs

Flight 1 compares **one video** with **three stills**. Same audience, $25 each. The video is the relocation film Devin posts on YouTube. The stills are free place photos, so the test is format (a person talking versus a place with type), not a photoshoot.

Build all four before November 1. Do not wait for a perfect vertical reshoot. A finished 25–35 second film beats another week of no ads.

## 1. Video — the YouTube relocation ad

**Cell:** `R-V1 Video` · copy A1 · `utm_content=video`

### What he posts

- Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@devindesaulniers
- Length: **25–35 seconds**. The homepage film already on the site is about 32 seconds, 1280×720, which is inside the length if that is the cut he posts.
- Watch URL (paste when it is live):

  `_______________________________________________`

- YouTube title, plain: `Moving to Greater Vancouver?`
- YouTube description, first line: the same landing URL as the ad, plus one sentence that the Tenant Relocation Service is $1,500 + GST, paid by the tenant. The organic description is not a substitute for the Meta file.

### What Meta gets

Download the file from **YouTube Studio** (the original upload) and upload that file in Ads Manager. A YouTube link-ad sends people to YouTube. This test needs a landing-page view on devindesaulniers.ca, so the video has to play inside Facebook and Instagram.

| | Preferred | Acceptable for November 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 25–35 seconds | Up to 45 seconds if the posted cut runs long. Do not hold the test for a trim. |
| Shape | 1080×1920 (9:16), subject in the centre | The 16:9 YouTube file. Feeds will play it. Reels and Stories will letterbox it. |
| Sound | Voice, little or no music | Same |
| Captions | Burned in. Many people watch muted. | Same. Auto-captions that cover his face are a fail. |
| End card | Name, REALTOR®, Axford Real Estate, `$1,500 + GST` | At least name and brokerage on screen |

Safe zones: keep his face and the captions out of the top 15% and the bottom 20%, where Reels puts buttons. If he only has 16:9, keep captions in the middle band.

### Script (~30 seconds)

On screen for the first three seconds, before the credentials: **MOVING TO GREATER VANCOUVER?**

Spoken:

> Moving to Greater Vancouver, but not sure where you should live?
>
> I’m Devin Desaulniers, a local REALTOR® with Axford Real Estate.
>
> I help people relocating here find a rental that fits their commute, budget, and the way they live — before they arrive.
>
> Moving from another province or another city? I can run the search here.
>
> Tap below and tell me your timing.

End card, on screen, not all of it spoken:

**Tenant Relocation Service · $1,500 + GST · paid by the tenant**
**Devin Desaulniers, REALTOR® · Axford Real Estate**

### Shot list

Use what he can film in an afternoon. Cut anything that makes it longer than 35 seconds.

1. Direct to camera, outdoors, first line. Face readable on a phone.
2. One wide shot of a place people recognize (inlet, SkyTrain, or a Tri-Cities street). Two seconds.
3. One rental-building exterior. No unit number, no client, no door code.
4. Back to camera for the close and the tap.

Skip market statistics, logo animations, and a montage of listings. The workbook’s rule holds: the relocation problem is obvious before the credentials, and there is one action at the end.

### Meta settings for this ad

- Creative: single video, the file above.
- Primary text, headline, description: copy A1.
- Captions: burned in, so a platform caption track is optional. If both exist, they must match.
- Thumbnail: his face, with the hook text already in the frame.
- Do not turn on Advantage+ creative that rewrites the headline, swaps the video, or generates a new frame. That breaks the even test.

## 2. Stills — three free photographs

Each still is one ad. Download the original from the page (the download button, not a screenshot). Put type on it. An unaltered stock photo is a weak ad, and the stock licences do not want the file resold as-is.

**Licence, both sources.** Free to use, including in ads. Attribution is not required; keep the photographer’s name in this folder anyway. Do not sell the photo by itself. Do not imply that a person in the frame endorses Devin. These three are places, which avoids that problem. Confirm the licence badge on the page the day he downloads.

- Pexels licence: https://www.pexels.com/license/
- Unsplash licence: https://unsplash.com/license/

**Do not** caption a photo as a listing, a “just listed” home, or “your new apartment.” None of these is a property he is offering.

### Shared layout (all three stills)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Size for the test | **1080 × 1350** (4:5). One size for every still, so shape is not the thing being tested. |
| Optional second file | 1080 × 1080, same words, if a placement crops the 4:5 badly. |
| Type | White, bold, sans serif (Montserrat or Arial). |
| Scrim | Dark gradient at the top and a solid bar at the bottom (`#132639`, the site’s ink). |
| Top line | The ad’s headline, two lines maximum. |
| Bottom bar | `Devin Desaulniers, REALTOR® · Axford Real Estate` |
| Fee | One line under the headline: `$1,500 + GST · paid by the tenant` |
| Margins | Keep all type at least 80 px inside the frame. |
| Logo | Wordmark in the bar is enough. The wide Axford logo fights a phone crop. |

Brand blue `#3474b6` can be a thin rule under the headline. Do not set small grey type on a photo; it will fail on a phone.

## What “video versus stills” can and cannot prove

With $25 an ad, the winner is a direction. It can show which of the four got a qualified inquiry at a lower cost. It cannot prove a provincial market, a buyer offer, or a forever creative. November 15 picks one ad to keep spending on. It does not pick a brand system.
