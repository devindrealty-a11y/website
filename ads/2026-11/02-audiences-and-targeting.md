# Audiences and targeting

Housing ads on Meta cannot be narrowed the way a normal campaign can. Declare **Special Ad Category: Housing** on the campaign before any location is set. Age and gender stay on Meta’s locked defaults (18–65+ and all genders). No postal codes, no lookalikes, no interest stacks, no “exclude families” or any other protected-class filter.

The headline does the qualifying: “Moving to Greater Vancouver?” Someone who is not moving scrolls past.

## Audience A — relocating renters (the $100 test)

This is the only audience that spends in November.

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| People | Adults living in **Canada, excluding British Columbia** |
| Why exclude B.C. | The offer is for people who still have to figure the region out. Local renters who already know the market, and only want free listing links, are the low-priority lead in the workbook. |
| If the exclusion is blocked | Target Canada and leave the exclusion off. Do not switch to postal codes to recreate it. The headline still has to do the work. |
| Radius | Meta’s Housing category enforces a wide radius (about 15 miles / 25 km). Accept that. Do not fight it with a tight pin. |
| Languages | English |
| Placements | Facebook and Instagram feeds, Reels, and Stories. Leave Audience Network off so the $25 units are not spent on junk inventory. |
| Detailed targeting | None |
| Objective | Traffic, optimized for landing page views. There is no Meta pixel on the site, so a Leads or Conversions objective would optimize on a guess. Qualified inquiries are counted by hand. |

Four ad sets, one ad each, **same settings**. Names:

1. `R-V1 Video`
2. `R-S1 Skyline`
3. `R-S2 SkyTrain`
4. `R-S3 Coquitlam`

Lifetime budget **$25** each. Schedule **November 1–14, 2026**.

Origin markets from the workbook (Ontario / Toronto, Calgary and Edmonton, Victoria and the Island, Kelowna and the Interior) are inside “Canada excluding B.C.” At $7 a day, splitting those provinces into separate ad sets would starve every cell. One geography. The copy can still say Ontario, Alberta, the Island, and the Interior.

International arrivals are a real client group. They are not a paid location in this test. A country-by-country buy would spend the $100 before anyone local to those cities saw the ad. The spoken line “or farther away” covers them when they are already in the Canadian geo (newcomers already in Canada, outside B.C.).

### Who counts as the right person

- Moving to Greater Vancouver, usually from outside the region.
- Does not have time to chase listings from another city.
- A move date that is real. For a rental, the active search is about 4–6 weeks out; planning further ahead can still be qualified.
- Will talk about a paid service. The fee is $1,500 + GST, paid by the tenant.

### Who is out, even if they click

- Already lives here and wants free links.
- Budget or building list that cannot be searched in good faith.
- Last-minute ask with requirements that cannot be met.
- Will not hear that the service is paid.

Those clicks can still happen. They are logged, and they are not qualified inquiries. See [06-conversion-tracking.md](06-conversion-tracking.md).

## Audience B — relocating buyers (written, not launched)

Same Housing rules and the same geography: Canada, excluding British Columbia, English, Facebook and Instagram only.

Use this audience only after the renter test has a winner, and only by **replacing** spend inside the $100–$200 month. Running buyers and renters together on this budget mixes two offers and two landing pages, and the November read stops meaning “video versus stills.”

Destination, when he does turn it on: `buyers.html#listings`. Copy is in [03-ad-copy.md](03-ad-copy.md). Buyer representation is a different service from the $1,500 tenant fee. The buyer ads do not quote that fee.

A renter who may buy in six to twelve months stays in Audience A. The relocating page already says he can help with both. That future purchase is pipeline. It is not counted as ad return until a buyer fee is actually received.

## Audience C — Google Search (optional, later)

Off in November. Off until the Meta winner is producing qualified inquiries. If it is turned on, the dollars come **out of** the $100–$200 month. They are not added on top.

Search ads need a different kind of match: the words are the audience.

| Ad group | Intent | Landing page | Starting keywords (phrase match) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relocation | Wants a person, not a listing feed | `relocating-to-greater-vancouver.html#rentals` | "relocation realtor vancouver", "relocation services vancouver", "moving to vancouver rental", "rental agent vancouver", "help finding apartment vancouver", "find me a rental vancouver" |
| Coquitlam rentals | Wants to browse rentals | `rentals.html` | "coquitlam rentals", "coquitlam apartments for rent", "port moody rentals", "port coquitlam rentals" |

The Coquitlam rentals group will attract local people comparing listings. That is a weaker fit for a $1,500 service. Keep it separate in the log. A rental-listing inquiry is not a Tenant Relocation Service sale.

Negative keywords for both groups: job, jobs, hiring, salary, career, course, template, pdf, law, “rent increase”, airbnb, hotel, hostel, roommate, sublet, free.

Location for a future Google test: Canada, or the same origin provinces, with the ad copy doing the “moving to” filter. Do not bid on Greater Vancouver alone for the relocation group; those searchers often already live here. The Coquitlam rentals group is the exception: it is a local listing search, and its location is Greater Vancouver.

Google’s housing policies apply when that test starts (personalized-advertising limits, no discriminatory copy). The same fee and brokerage lines as the Meta ads.

## What not to target

- Age bands, gender, income, family status, or “newcomers” as a demographic.
- Postal codes, a single SkyTrain station, or a radius small enough to pick a building.
- Homeowners versus renters as a saved audience. Housing category will not allow the usual detailed targeting, and the creative is the filter.
- Retargeting site visitors. There is no pixel on the site, and this package does not add one.
- A lookalike of past clients. Not available in the usual way under Housing, and the client list is not the test.
