# Ad copy

Four variants for each audience. Every variant names **Devin Desaulniers** and **Axford Real Estate**. The fee, when the ad states it, matches the site: **$1,500 + GST**, paid by the tenant, **$750 at the start (non-refundable)** and **$750 once a rental is secured**.

Paste these as written. Do not add “guaranteed”, “best”, “#1”, “free buyer agent”, “off-market”, or “exclusive access”. Do not put a review quote or a review count in an ad. The Google counts on the site were checked in early October 2026 and will have moved by November.

`REALTOR®` is the CREA mark. Keep the ® on the first use in each ad.

Character limits used here: Meta headline 40, Meta description 30, Google headline 30, Google description 90. A checker for this file is at the bottom of [launch-checklist.md](launch-checklist.md).

UTMs for each variant are in [landing-pages.md](landing-pages.md).

- If a rental is listed or managed by Axford, he discloses that in writing before the client commits
- Direct phone 604-809-1032

## Claims to leave out

- Any promise that a rental or a purchase will be found
- “Early access” or “off-market” as a headline (the site’s “early access” means Axford’s own rentals and property-manager relationships, which is too easy to over-read in an ad)
- MLS photos, listing prices, or street addresses
- Other agents’ listings presented as Devin’s
- A buyer commission number, a mortgage rate, or “you’re approved”
- Axford’s 148 five-star count, or Devin’s 15-review count, unless someone re-checks Google that week and the brokerage approves the exact sentence
- William Chen’s review, which the site already excludes
- Immigration help, movers, or property management (the site says he does not do those)
