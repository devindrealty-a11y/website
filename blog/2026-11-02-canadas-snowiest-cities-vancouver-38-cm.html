Canada's snowiest cities. Vancouver? Just 38 cm a year

St. John's gets about 335 cm of snow in an average year. Vancouver gets just 38 cm, the lowest of 15 Canadian cities. Here's what that means if you're relocating, and why the hillsides are a different story.

It's November, so let's talk snow. ❄️

This started as a Halloween post on my social channels on Saturday. Here's that version, with a bit more room to explain the numbers.

How much snow Canadian cities get

In an average year, St. John's gets about 335 cm of snow. Quebec City gets 303. Ottawa 223, Calgary 129, Toronto 108.

Vancouver? Just 38 cm. That's the lowest of the 15 Canadian cities on this map, and about one-ninth of what St. John's shovels.

Here's the full ranking, in centimetres of snow in an average year:

  1. St. John's, N.L.: 335
  2. Quebec City, Que.: 303
  3. Charlottetown, P.E.I.: 290
  4. Fredericton, N.B.: 252
  5. Ottawa, Ont.: 223
  6. Halifax, N.S.: 221
  7. Montreal, Que.: 210
  8. Calgary, Alta.: 129
  9. Edmonton, Alta.: 118
  10. Winnipeg, Man.: 114
  11. Toronto, Ont.: 108
  12. Regina, Sask.: 100
  13. Saskatoon, Sask.: 91
  14. Victoria, B.C.: 40
  15. Vancouver, B.C.: 38

These are Environment and Climate Change Canada's Climate Normals for 1981–2010, measured at each city's airport station (Regina, Saskatoon and Winnipeg cover 1981–2007, and Fredericton 1981–2008).

Why it matters for a move

  • Coming from Ottawa or Montreal, you're trading close to 2 metres of snow a year for a good rain jacket ☔
  • Fewer snow days means less wear and tear on driveways, roofs and your back
  • Forecasters say this year's super El Niño should bring a milder winter than usual, too

But there's another side to it

That 38 cm is measured at YVR, right at sea level. Up on Westwood Plateau or Burke Mountain in Coquitlam it's a different story, so good tires and a solid driveway still matter when you're house hunting on the hillsides of the Tri-Cities, BC.

My take

Our winters are a real selling point for people relocating here. Just budget for the rain gear.
