Coquitlam neighbourhoods for newcomers: three different streets

Coquitlam neighbourhoods for newcomers are three different streets: Burke Mountain is still being planned, Westwood Plateau replaced a racetrack in 1990, and Maillardville keeps a 1909 mill-town pattern.

Coquitlam neighbourhoods for newcomers are not one kind of street. Burke Mountain is the hillside the City is still planning in pieces, Westwood Plateau is the hill that replaced a racetrack, and Maillardville is the old mill town in the southwest.

I'm a lifelong Coquitlam local, and I've lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years, leasing across the Tri-Cities, BC. Burke Mountain

Burke Mountain is several plans, not one street grid. The Northwest Burke Vision, adopted on July 17, 2017, covers about 400 hectares. The City calls it the last large undeveloped area in Coquitlam, with Pinecone Burke Provincial Park to the north and east. South of that, the 2026 Official Community Plan, the City's long-range land plan, already includes Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek.

Partington Creek is about 240 hectares, a walk or a short bike or scooter ride from Pinecone Burke Provincial Park and Minnekhada Regional Park.

The timetable effective September 7, 2026, runs the 188 from Coquitlam Central along David Avenue and Coast Meridian Road to Port Coquitlam Station, past Lincoln and Lafarge Lake–Douglas. The 191 loops to Princeton on David Avenue. Smiling Creek Elementary is at 3456 Princeton Avenue. School District 43 describes a combined middle and secondary school here. In August 2026 its capital list put the work at about 70 percent, planned for fall 2027, and said that date can be revised. On August 24, 2026 the Province said the school is set to open in 2027.

Westwood Plateau

Westwood Plateau is already built. City history says the Westwood Racing Circuit opened in 1959 at the end of Johnson Street on Eagle Mountain, calls it the first purpose-built permanent road course in Canada, and says it closed in 1990 for this housing. It is not one of the eight neighbourhood plans.

The 186 ends at Paddock on Plateau Boulevard, via Lafarge Lake–Douglas. The 187 uses Johnson Street and Plateau Boulevard. École Panorama Heights Elementary is at 1455 Johnson Street, and Pinetree Secondary is at 3000 Pinewood Avenue. Use the school locator. The station below the hill is in my Lafarge Lake–Douglas guide.

Maillardville

Maillardville is the old village. The same history says the kʷikʷəƛ̓əm people have lived on these lands since time immemorial. In 1909, 110 French Canadians arrived to work at Fraser Mills, and a second group arrived in June 1910. The City calls that community the largest Francophone centre west of Manitoba.

The Maillardville plan calls it the City's most historic neighbourhood, about 370 hectares, between Highway 1 and Rochester Avenue, with Lougheed Highway and Braid Street SkyTrain nearby. Laval Square and Allard-LeBleu keep early-1900s houses, some built by mill workers with lumber from the mill. On Brunette Avenue, buildings over eight storeys are allowed west of LeBleu Street.

Maillardville Community Centre, at 1200 Cartier Avenue, is 22,000 square feet. The City lists buses 153, 156 and 157. The 157 runs between Burquitlam Station and Lougheed Station on LeBleu, Alderson, Marmont, Rochester and Schoolhouse. Alderson Elementary is at 825 Gauthier Avenue, and École Maillard Middle is at 1300 Rochester Avenue.

Is Burke Mountain one neighbourhood?

No. The City plans it as Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek, plus the Northwest Burke Vision for about 400 hectares further north, beside Pinecone Burke Provincial Park.

Which of these three is a walk to SkyTrain?

None of them. Burquitlam and City Centre are the walk-to-station parts of Coquitlam. Burke Mountain, Westwood Plateau and Maillardville mean a bus or a car for the train.

How do I find the school for a Coquitlam address?

Use School District 43's school locator. Catchment is set by the address, and it can change. A Burke Mountain middle and secondary school is planned for fall 2027, and the district says that date can be revised.
