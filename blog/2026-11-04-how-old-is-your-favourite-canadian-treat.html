How old is your favourite Canadian treat? The butter tart turns 126

🍁 How old is your favourite Canadian treat?

This one went up on my social channels yesterday, and it got people talking. Here it is with a little more room for the full list.

The short version

The butter tart's first published recipe dates to 1900, so it turns 126 this year. Coffee Crisp is 88. Kraft Dinner hit Canadian shelves in 1937. Timbits only showed up in 1976, and BeaverTails in 1978.

B.C. makes the list too: the first recipe printed under the name "Nanaimo bar" came out in 1953.

The full timeline

Here's when 14 iconic Canadian treats were first introduced (first launch or earliest published recipe):

Before 1930

  • Butter tart: 1900 (earliest published recipe)
  • Laura Secord: 1913
  • Pal-O-Mine: 1920

1930 to 1949

  • Jos. Louis: 1932
  • Smarties: 1937
  • Kraft Dinner: 1937
  • Coffee Crisp: 1938
  • Shreddies: 1939
  • Cheezies: 1949

1950 to 1969

  • Nanaimo bar: 1953 (B.C.!)
  • Poutine: around 1959
  • Caesar: 1969

1970 to 1979

  • Timbits: 1976
  • BeaverTails: 1978

Years come from each product's history on Wikipedia (linked in the sources below).

A tip for newcomers

New to B.C.? Grab a Nanaimo bar on your first house-hunting weekend. It's basically a rite of passage. 😄
