The seven items from the caption

1. Medical Services Plan (MSP). Enrol early. The Province of B.C. explains the coverage wait period for new residents. If you're moving from another province, keep your old plan during the wait.

2. ICBC. ICBC sets the deadlines for switching your driver's licence and registering a vehicle when you move to B.C. Book the appointment before the clock runs out.

3. BC Hydro. Open your electricity account so the power is in your name on move-in day. If the home uses natural gas, set up FortisBC too, and book internet while you're at it.

4. Tenant insurance. Many landlords require it. Get a policy that fits your stuff and your lease before you unpack everything.

5. Compass Card. TransLink uses a reloadable Compass Card for SkyTrain, buses and SeaBus. In the Tri-Cities, BC, the Millennium Line (Evergreen Extension) is the everyday link into Burnaby and Vancouver.

6. School. Families in Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Anmore and Belcarra register with School District No. 43 (Coquitlam). Start that paperwork early if kids need a seat for the current school year.

7. A family doctor. Once you have your Personal Health Number, use the Health Connect Registry or call 8-1-1. Then, as the TikTok says, buy a real rain jacket and go outside.
