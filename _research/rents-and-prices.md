### GVR sales and inventory claims

The 2 October GVR release verifies:

- **1,717 sales**, down **8.4%** from **1,875** in September 2025 and **25% below** the 10-year September average of **2,289**.
- **575 detached sales**, up **4.2%** from 552; **358 attached sales**, up **0.6%** from 356; and **777 apartment sales**, down **18.6%** from 954.
- **5,852 new listings**, down **10.3%** from 6,527, and **16,394 active listings**, down **4%** from 17,079 but **24.3% above** the 10-year September average of 13,186.
- Sales-to-active-listings ratios of **10.9% overall**, **9.7% detached**, **12.2% attached**, and **11.4% apartments**.
- GVR's historical-data interpretation: downward price pressure can occur when the ratio remains below **12%** for a sustained period; upward pressure often occurs when it remains above **20%** for several months.

All supplied GVR headline claims match the release. One source-level detail should be preserved: the release calls the overall monthly composite decline **0.6%**, while the HPI table displays **-0.5%**; this research does not silently reconcile that rounding discrepancy.

## 3. British Columbia property transfer tax

### General rates

Source: [Province of British Columbia — Property transfer tax](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax), last updated **3 December 2025** and fetched 11 October 2026.

Property transfer tax is based on fair market value when the interest is registered, unless an exemption or special rule applies:

- **1%** on fair market value up to and including **$200,000**.
- **2%** on the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including **$2,000,000**.
- **3%** on the portion greater than **$2,000,000**.
- A further **2%** applies to the residential-property-value portion greater than **$3,000,000**.

### First-time home buyers' program

Sources: [program rules](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers), last updated **20 June 2025**, and [current exemption amounts](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers/current-amount), last updated **3 December 2025**.

For registrations on or after 1 April 2024, where 100% of the transfer qualifies and the property is no larger than 0.5 hectares:

- Fair market value **$500,000 or less**: exemption equals the full ordinary property transfer tax.
- Over $500,000 through **$835,000**: exemption is **$8,000**. At $835,000, ordinary tax is $14,700 and tax payable after the exemption is $6,700.
- Over $835,000 and under **$860,000**: the exemption phases out proportionally.
- At **$860,000**, the exemption is $0.

Core purchaser requirements include Canadian citizenship or permanent residence; the specified B.C. residence/tax-filing test; never having owned a registered interest in a principal residence anywhere in the world; and never having received this exemption or refund. The property must be used only as the principal residence. Property size, non-residential improvements, and the percentage held by qualifying purchasers can reduce the exemption.

### Newly built home exemption

Sources: [newly built home exemption](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption), last updated **6 January 2026**, and [current exemption amounts](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption/exemption-amounts), last updated **3 December 2025**.

For qualifying principal residences registered on or after 1 April 2024:

- Fair market value up to **$1,100,000**: full ordinary property transfer tax exemption.
- More than $1,100,000 and less than **$1,150,000**: proportional partial exemption.
- At **$1,150,000**, the exemption is $0.

The property generally must be in B.C., be 0.5 hectares or smaller, be used only as the purchaser's principal residence, and meet the first-registration/completed-improvement and purchaser-status rules on the official page. The program remains in force as of 11 October 2026. The official page publishes **no sunset or end date**; it states that the exemption applies to qualifying title registrations after 16 February 2016.

### Additional property transfer tax for foreign entities

Source: [Province of British Columbia — Additional property transfer tax for foreign entities and taxable trustees](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/additional-property-transfer-tax), last updated **20 June 2025**.

The rate is **20%** of the fair market value of the foreign national's, foreign corporation's, or taxable trustee's proportionate share of the residential portion of property in:

- Capital Regional District
- Fraser Valley Regional District
- Metro Vancouver Regional District
- Regional District of Central Okanagan
- Regional District of Nanaimo

The official page says the additional tax does not apply on Tsawwassen First Nation treaty lands and lists limited exemptions, including qualifying confirmed B.C. Provincial Nominees and certain Canadian-controlled limited partnerships.
