Unpublished. Do not turn these on until Devin approves the guides.

Advertiser: Devin Desaulniers, Real Estate Associate, Axford Real Estate.
Destination for every ad: `https://devindesaulniers.ca/free-guide.html`
Optional deep links (pre-selects the guide on the form):

- Greater Vancouver: `https://devindesaulniers.ca/free-guide.html?guide=greater-vancouver`
- Tri-Cities: `https://devindesaulniers.ca/free-guide.html?guide=tri-cities`

Creatives are in `_promo/creatives/`. Each file is 1080×1080 or 1080×1920. Every creative shows the words **Axford Real Estate**.

## Housing special ad category

Run both campaigns in Meta’s **Housing** special ad category.

- Do not restrict age, gender, or postal code. Special-ad audiences cannot use those exclusions.
- Geographic targeting, if used, should be a broad region (for example British Columbia, or Canada) rather than a demographic slice.
- Copy stays factual: a free guide for people moving to a place. It does not say who should live there.
- Words avoided on purpose: family, families, kids, couples, young professionals, exclusive, luxury, safe neighbourhood, and any “best” or “top” ranking.

CTA values below are Meta’s button labels.
