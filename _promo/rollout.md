# Four-week rollout

Start the week of October 13, 2026, or the next Monday after Axford Real Estate's managing broker has read the ad text. Dates below assume a Monday start. Shift the whole grid if the approval lands later. Do not spend money in a week the broker has not signed off.

Budget for the paid test: **$8 a day, $56 a week**, inside the $5–$10 band in `meta-ads.md`. At $5 a day, run only the rental campaign and skip the campaign 2 and 3 tasks.

A lead means a tenant intake, a buyer listings request, or a call or text from a person who is moving, buying, or selling. The forms email `devin@axfordrealestate.ca` and do not attach the UTM. Count the inbox against that week's spend.

## Week 1 — set up, one campaign, first organic posts

- [ ] Managing broker reads `meta-ads.md` (all nine ads), the YouTube footer, and community drafts 1 and 2.
- [ ] Confirm the Facebook Page and Instagram account are connected, and that a business admin has accepted Meta's non-discrimination policy.
- [ ] Create campaign `relocation-rentals` only. Special ad category: Housing. Country: Canada. Audience: Calgary, Edmonton, Toronto, Ottawa, Montréal, Winnipeg, Halifax. Age 18–65+, all genders, no detailed targeting. Placements: Facebook Feed and Instagram Feed. Budget: $4 a day if you are on the $8 plan, or $5 if that is the whole account. The other $4 of an $8 day stays unspent this week so the first CPC is readable. (If you would rather not leave it unspent, add it to this campaign and note that you did.)
- [ ] Load ads c1-v1, c1-v2, and c1-v3 with the URLs in `meta-ads.md`. Check the preview: "Axford Real Estate" is visible before "See more," and it is not cropped off the blog-card footer.
- [ ] Publish Google Business Profile post 1 (rent before you move). Button: Learn more. URL from `google-business-posts.md`.
- [ ] Paste the six YouTube descriptions and pin the six comments. One sitting, about an hour. Ids are in `youtube.md`.
- [ ] Open r/vancouver and confirm the monthly promotions thread is the current stickied one. Post draft 1 there, and only there.
- [ ] Join the four Facebook groups in `communities.md` and read their rules. Post nothing yet.
- [ ] Friday: write down spend, link clicks, CPC, and leads for campaign 1.

## Week 2 — buyers campaign, second post, rules check

- [ ] Monday: apply the stop rules in `meta-ads.md` to the three rental ads. Pause any ad that spent $20 with fewer than 5 link clicks.
- [ ] If an ad was paused, leave the other two running. Do not write a new ad this week.
- [ ] Turn on campaign `tri-cities-buyers` at $2.50 a day. Pin: 2326 Clarke St, Port Moody, radius 25 km. Housing category. Ads c3-v1, c3-v2, c3-v3. Same placement limits. Brokerage name in the first 125 characters.
- [ ] Leave campaign `relocating` off. Week 2 is two campaigns, not three.
- [ ] Move the rental campaign to $4 a day if week 1 was at $5, so the pair fits $8 (rental $4, buyers $2.50, $1.50 held for the guide next week). If you prefer round numbers, rental $5 and buyers $3 is the $8 plan with the guide still off. Write down which split you used.
- [ ] Publish Google post 2 (deposits and the written agreement).
- [ ] If a Facebook group's rules allow a source link, post draft 2 in that one group. If every group bans links or business posts, do not post. Note the rule you read, in one line, so you are not re-deciding next month.
- [ ] Reply to any comment on the YouTube pins or the Reddit thread. Answer the question first.
- [ ] Friday: spend, clicks, CPC, and leads for both campaigns.

## Week 3 — guide campaign, and the first real stop

- [ ] Monday: pause a buyers ad on the same $20 / 5-click rule, or if its CPC is more than double the other buyers ad after $15.
- [ ] Turn on campaign `relocating` at $1.50 a day with ads c2-v1, c2-v2, and c2-v3, only if campaign 1 has a CPC you can compare (at least 5 clicks on one rental ad). If campaign 1 has not reached 5 clicks, skip the guide and add the $1.50 to the rental campaign.
- [ ] If campaign 1 has now spent $50 in any seven days with zero leads, pause it and move that money to the buyers campaign for this week. That is the stop rule, not a judgment call.
- [ ] Publish Google post 3 (rental scams).
- [ ] Do not post another Reddit promotion if draft 1 was this month. One promotion in the monthly thread is enough. If the new month's thread is up and draft 1 was last month, you may post draft 3 instead.
- [ ] Modmail r/PortCoquitlam with the draft 5 note. Do not post draft 5 until they answer.
- [ ] Friday numbers, plus a one-line note on lead quality: was the person actually moving or buying, and did they have a date?

## Week 4 — decide, don't decorate

- [ ] Monday: if the guide's CPC is higher than the rental campaign's after $25, turn the guide off and move its $1.50 a day to whichever of rental or buyers produced a lead. If neither produced a lead, turn the guide off anyway and do not replace it.
- [ ] Scale only if one campaign has at least 2 real leads in 14 days at under $40 each. Add $1 a day, up to a $10 ceiling, and do not touch it again this week.
- [ ] If the account has passed $150 with zero real conversations, stop all three campaigns. Tell the managing broker. Do not launch new creative to "save" the test.
- [ ] Publish Google post 4 (September asking rents).
- [ ] If r/PortCoquitlam approved draft 5, post it. If they have not answered, leave it.
- [ ] Queue Google posts 5–8 on the calendar (November 10, November 17, November 24, December 1, or the same weekday shifted to your real start). They are already written.
- [ ] Write a half page for the broker: spend, CPC by ad, leads, which ads you paused, and whether you are staying at $8, moving to $10, or stopping.

## After week 4

Posts 5 through 8 go out weekly. They do not need a new ad campaign. Repeat a Reddit draft only in a new month's promotions thread, and not the same draft two months in a row. Re-read the group rules on the day you post. The September figures in the ads and in posts 4, 5, and 8 age out. When the next Greater Vancouver REALTORS® package or the next Rentals.ca report is the one you want to talk about, retire the ad that cites the old number instead of letting it run.
