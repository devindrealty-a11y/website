The Bank of Canada raised the policy rate on Wednesday by a quarter point, from 2.25% to 2.50%. The new rate takes effect Thursday. September inflation is still 3.1%.

BUYERS

Variable payments, and many lines of credit, usually follow. On a $600,000 mortgage with 25 years left, a move from 4.50% to 4.75% is about $84 a month. Some buyers will qualify for a little less. Rerun the numbers before you write.

SELLERS

Around the Tri-Cities, showings can slow, especially on condos. Price for the people who can still qualify. One quarter point is not a collapse. In September the typical Metro Vancouver home was $1,075,900, already 5.5% below a year earlier.

Fixed rates follow bond yields, which often move before the announcement. A fixed rate you were already quoted may not change on Thursday.
