What Wednesday can do

The Bank of Canada announces Wednesday at 9:45 a.m. Eastern, which is 6:45 a.m. here. The policy rate is 2.25%. If it changes, the Bank says the new rate takes effect the next day. The Monetary Policy Report comes out the same morning.

The 3.1% in the box above is September inflation, from Statistics Canada on October 19. That is the latest reading going into the decision. One announcement does not reset prices on its own.

CUT

The rate would move below 2.25%. Variable-rate payments, and many lines of credit, usually ease a little. Fixed rates follow bond yields, which often move before Wednesday morning. A few more buyers may qualify. Prices do not jump just because the rate did.

HOLD

The rate stays at 2.25%. Variable payments stay where they are, so you can plan with the budget you already have. If you are selling, price from recent sales, not from a cut that has not happened.

HIKE

Variable payments can go up, and some buyers qualify for less or wait. Showings can slow, especially on condos. If you need to sell, price for the people who are looking now. One hike is not a collapse.

September prices, close to home

Greater Vancouver REALTORS counted 1,717 sales in September, 8.4% fewer than a year earlier. The drop was in apartments. Detached and townhouse sales were a little higher. The composite benchmark for Metro Vancouver, the board’s estimate for a typical home, was $1,075,900, 5.5% below September 2025.
