Privacy

How this site handles your information

What happens to the information you send through the contact forms, a listing inquiry, and the chat widget, and who to contact with a privacy question or request.

This site is operated by Devin Desaulniers, Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate, 2326 Clarke St, Port Moody, BC V3H 1Y8. The sections below explain, in plain language, where the information you send me goes.

Contact forms

There are six forms on this site: the “Send me listings” form on the buyers page, the relocation rental intake on the tenants page, the free home evaluation, the expert opinion on a property you're considering, and the inquiry on a home at homes for sale or homes for rent. When you press Send, the form is sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which emails your submission to devin@axfordrealestate.ca.

The forms ask for:

  • your name and email, and your phone number if you add it;
  • on the home evaluation and the expert opinion forms: your email, the property address, and your name if you add it;
  • on the listings form: the areas you're interested in, property type, price range, bedrooms and bathrooms, your timeline, whether you're pre-approved for a mortgage, and any notes;
  • on the rental intake: your target move-in date, how you'd like to be contacted, where you're moving from, your monthly rent budget, bedrooms, preferred areas, pets, your work or school location, and any notes;
  • on a listing or rental inquiry: the listing id, MLS® number, address and REALTOR.ca link for the home you opened, and your message.

Each form asks you to agree that I can contact you by email or phone about your request. You can opt out at any time.

Homes for sale and homes for rent

The homes on homes for sale and homes for rent come from the REALTOR.ca DDF® feed. Opening a listing sends a view to the CREA DDF® Analytics Web Service at analytics.crea.ca. That view includes the listing id, this site's DDF destination id, and the page address. The DDF rules require it. Listing photos load from the photo addresses in the feed.

Chat widget

Pages on this site include a chat widget, “Devin's AI Assistant”, provided by Elfsight. What you type into that chat is handled by Elfsight.

Processing outside Canada

Information you send through FormSubmit or the Elfsight chat widget may be processed outside Canada. If you'd rather not use the forms or the chat, you can call or text me at 604-809-1032 or email devin@axfordrealestate.ca.

Video tours

The YouTube video tours on this site show a preview image from YouTube, and the YouTube player itself loads only after you press play.
