Choosing

When each option makes sense

Hire a relocation rental agent

If you can't view homes in person, you're new to Canada or BC tenancy rules, or you need a place by a fixed start date.

Use a locator or relocation company

If your employer is paying for a package that already includes one. Ask who they represent and whether they're licensed.

Go DIY

If you're already in the Lower Mainland, can view in person, and are comfortable with BC tenancy agreements.

FAQ

Relocation rental questions

What does a relocation rental agent do in Vancouver?

A relocation rental agent finds rentals that fit your budget, area and move date, does live video walkthroughs for you, checks that the listing and landlord are real, helps with your application, and reviews and negotiates the lease before you sign.

Is a relocation rental agent the same as a rental locator?

Not always. A tenant-side relocation rental agent like me is a licensed REALTOR® who represents you, the tenant. Some locator services are unlicensed or are paid by landlords, so it's worth asking who they work for.

How much does a relocation rental agent cost in Vancouver?

My tenant service fee is $1,500 total + GST, paid by you, the tenant, in two parts: $750 at the beginning of the engagement (non-refundable) and $750 once you successfully secure a rental. Other services charge differently, so compare what's included.

Can you help me rent in Coquitlam, Port Moody or Port Coquitlam, not just Vancouver?

Yes. I'm based in the Tri-Cities, BC and help tenants across Greater Vancouver, including Coquitlam, Port Moody, Port Coquitlam, Burnaby and Vancouver.

Do I need to be in Canada to start?

No. We can start the search and do showings by live video while you're still out of town.
