How to use this guide

I’m Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate on Clarke Street in Port Moody. I’ve lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years, and I play soccer on a Port Coquitlam team. This guide is the version I wish people had before they wired a deposit from another province.

Greater Vancouver is a set of cities, not one city with neighbourhoods. The number that matters is your commute plus the rent or price on a specific home.

Two rent statistics get mixed up. Asking rents are vacant listings (Rentals.ca, September 2026, published 7 October 2026). CMHC averages include homes people already occupy (October 2025 survey). If you are arriving now, budget the asking rent.

The Tri-Cities edition — Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Anmore and Belcarra — is the companion to this one.

Big figures are Compass stored value, the cheaper adult fare. Still three zones in October 2026, and not a distance fare. Every bus is 1-zone, all day. SkyTrain is 1, 2 or 3 zones on a weekday daytime, and 1-zone after 6:30 p.m. and all weekend and holidays. SeaBus is 1 or 2 zones. Adult DayPass $12.55, not valid on the West Coast Express. Children 12 and under ride free. A fare lasts 90 minutes, or 120 if the West Coast Express is included.

Compass, briefly

The card is a $6 refundable deposit. Buy it at compasscard.ca, 604-398-2042, station vending machines, Waterfront’s customer service centre, or some London Drugs. Concession cards are not in the machines. Load stored value in amounts of at least $5.

West Coast Express

Its own zones, up to five. Moody Centre, Coquitlam Central and Port Coquitlam are 3 West Coast Express zones from Waterfront. A SkyTrain pass is not a West Coast Express pass; a West Coast Express return or monthly does include bus, SkyTrain and SeaBus that day. Parking was listed at $3 a day or $60 a month.

Day to day

Shopping and parks

Shopping

Downtown has street retail and Pacific Centre. Metrotown and Richmond Centre sit on SkyTrain. In the Tri-Cities, Coquitlam Centre is the enclosed mall — about 930,000 square feet, nearly 200 stores, at Lincoln station — with anchors including Walmart, Best Buy, London Drugs, T&T and UNIQLO.

Suter Brook Village and Newport Village in Port Moody are outdoor village streets, not malls. Suter Brook’s tenants have included Thrifty Foods. Rocky Point Ice Cream, inside Rocky Point Park, was still open when this was checked in October 2026.

Parks

Stanley Park’s seawall and Kitsilano Beach are the city walks. Buntzen Lake, in Anmore, is a BC Hydro site: parking is free, but recent summers (roughly mid-May to early September) have required a free next-day parking reservation or you are turned away. Walkers, cyclists and the 182 bus do not need one. The 182 stops at Anmore Grocery, about a 30-minute walk to South Beach.

Belcarra Regional Park (təmtəmíxʷtən) has Sasamat Lake and the Admiralty Point Trail, about 6 km. Mundy Park is Coquitlam’s 178-hectare urban forest with a 5.5 km loop. Town Centre Park is 42 hectares on Lafarge Lake. Burke Mountain’s city loops are short; Pinecone Burke’s backcountry has poor infrastructure, according to BC Parks.

Day to day

Schools, healthcare, and the setup

Schools

The district follows the address. SD39 Vancouver, SD41 Burnaby, SD40 New Westminster, SD38 Richmond, SD44 North Vancouver, SD36 Surrey, SD43 Coquitlam (Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Anmore, Belcarra). Confirm catchment on the district locator. SD43 secondary names on its own 2025–26 list, not a ranking: Centennial, Dr. Charles Best, Gleneagle, Heritage Woods, Pinetree, Port Moody Secondary, Riverside, Terry Fox.

Healthcare

Apply for MSP when you arrive. The wait is the rest of that month plus two months, unless you are a returning Canadian, a permanent resident, arriving under CUEAT, or a discharged CAF family — those waits are waived. Keep other insurance for the gap. Register for a family doctor on the Health Connect Registry; the Province will not estimate the wait. Eagle Ridge Hospital in Port Moody (475 Guildford Way, 604-461-2022) is the Tri-Cities community hospital. Royal Columbian in New Westminster is Fraser Health’s Level 1 trauma centre.

Licence, power, internet

Exchange an out-of-province licence within 90 days at an ICBC office. Bring the licence, ID, and proof of driving history. BC Hydro is electricity; open the account up to 60 days ahead. The first bill includes $13.50 + GST, and some accounts need a deposit. FortisBC is natural gas across the Tri-Cities. Internet: TELUS, Rogers (the former Shaw network), and Novus in some buildings only.

Before you pay anyone · Residential Tenancy Act, checked 11 October 2026

Renting rules

½ monthSecurity deposit

At most half a month’s rent. Pet damage deposit, if any, at most another half month. Not first-and-last.

$0Application fee

Application fees are illegal under section 15 of the Residential Tenancy Act, including a “credit-check fee.”

2.3%2026 increase cap

2.2% in 2027. Once every 12 months, with three full months’ notice on the proper form.

7 / 15days · RTB-27

Condition inspection at move-in and move-out. You get a copy within 7 days of move-in and 15 of move-out.

$100Dispute filing

Residential Tenancy Branch filing fee. Phone 1‑800‑665‑8779.

4–6weeks out

Most listings appear only a few weeks before move-in. A serious search usually starts 4–6 weeks out.

Before you send money

See the home, in person or on a live video from inside it.

Confirm the person can rent it. Be wary of a price far under the asking rents on page , of pressure to pay today, and of wire, gift cards or crypto. Half a month is the legal deposit ceiling, and nobody may charge you to apply.

If you buy · Province of B.C., page last updated 3 December 2025

Property transfer tax

Charged on fair market value at registration. The rate steps up through the price; each band is taxed at its own rate.

1%on the first $200,000
2%from $200,000 to $2,000,000
3%above $2,000,000
+2%further, on the residential portion above $3,000,000

First-time buyers’ exemption

Full exemption at $500,000 or under. It phases out to zero at $860,000. You generally must be a citizen or permanent resident, meet the residence test, never have owned a principal residence anywhere, and live in the home. The lot is generally 0.5 hectares or smaller for the full amount.

Two more lines

Foreign buyers can owe an additional 20% on their share of residential property in Metro Vancouver; exemptions exist. GST on a new home is a separate question I am not calculating here. None of this is tax advice.

Things locals know

“The city” is ambiguous. It can mean Vancouver or the whole region.

PoCo is not Coquitlam. Different city, no SkyTrain.

Evergreen is not a transfer. It is the Millennium Line.

West Coast Express is one-way at rush hour. Not a midday SkyTrain.

After 6:30 p.m., long SkyTrain trips are 1-zone. Buy the pass for the daytime trip.

Half a month, not first and last. And no application fee.

The neighbour’s rent is not your rent. CMHC includes sitting tenants. Listings do not.

November is the wet month. 245 mm at Vancouver Harbour, on average.

Buntzen in summer needs a parking pass. Free, and still mandatory in recent seasons.

A listing does not assign a school. The district’s locator does.

MSP does not start on landing day for most newcomers.

Anmore and Belcarra are villages. The nearest rapid transit is in Port Moody.
