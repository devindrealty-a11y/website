Moving to Greater Vancouver

The insider’s guide. Rents, benchmark prices, SkyTrain, schools, MSP, and the first 30 days.

I’m Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate on Clarke Street in Port Moody. I’ve lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years, and I play soccer on a Port Coquitlam team. This guide is the version I wish people had before they wired a deposit from another province.

Greater Vancouver is a set of cities, not one city with neighbourhoods. The number that matters is your commute plus the rent or price on a specific home. Two rent statistics get mixed up. Asking rents are vacant listings (Rentals.ca, September 2026, published 7 October 2026). CMHC averages include homes people already occupy (October 2025 survey). If you are arriving now, budget the asking rent.

Figures change. Benchmarks are Greater Vancouver REALTORS® for September 2026, published 2 October 2026. Surrey is the Fraser Valley board, 5 October 2026. Tax and tenancy rules were checked 11 October 2026. This is general information, not legal, tax or mortgage advice.

Where to liveNeighbourhood snapshots

Vancouver

The city proper

Downtown and the West End are towers and seawall. Kitsilano is further west, by the beach. Mount Pleasant, Fairview and Olympic Village sit in the middle. East Vancouver is lower-rise. GVR splits the city: September 2026 composite benchmark $1,120,200 east and $1,206,600 west. Expo, Millennium and Canada lines.

Burnaby

Three town centres

Metrotown (Expo), Brentwood (Millennium) and Lougheed (both lines) are the tower clusters. GVR reports Burnaby as East, North and South. September composites: $982,400, $877,100 and $972,800. Lougheed is the last stop before the Tri-Cities.

New Westminster

Compact, on the Expo Line

Downtown on the Fraser, houses on the hill. September composite $719,600, apartment benchmark $579,900 — the lowest composite in the GVR table in this guide.

Richmond

Canada Line and the airport

Apartment benchmark $635,600. Apartment and condo asking rents averaged $2,572, down 8.4% year over year. Trips that start at YVR–Airport, Sea Island Centre or Templeton add a $6.50 AddFare.

North Shore

Bridges or the SeaBus

The City and the District of North Vancouver are different municipalities. September asking rents for North Vancouver apartments and condos averaged $2,983, described as essentially unchanged year over year. SeaBus runs Lonsdale Quay to Waterfront, a 1- or 2-zone fare. Lions Gate or Ironworkers if you drive.

Tri-Cities

Where I work

Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, plus Anmore and Belcarra. September composites: Coquitlam $971,700, PoCo $835,500, Port Moody $1,011,500. SkyTrain through Coquitlam and Port Moody. PoCo is West Coast Express, not SkyTrain. Detail is in the Tri-Cities guide.

Surrey and Langley

A different real estate board

Fraser Valley board, City of Surrey combined (excluding White Rock): detached $1,386,200, townhouse $758,600, apartment $447,600. No composite on that line. Expo Line is in Surrey. The Surrey–Langley SkyTrain extension is under construction — confirm the opening date before you rely on a future station. Langley City and the Township are separate in the CMHC table.

Pitt Meadows & Maple Ridge

Up the Fraser

West Coast Express stops at Pitt Meadows, Maple Meadows and Port Haney, weekday rush hour only. The R3 RapidBus runs from Coquitlam Central to Haney Place. I am not quoting a benchmark I did not verify for these cities.

Straight lines, TransLink’s station order, not to scale. The Evergreen Extension is the east end of the Millennium Line. You do not transfer onto a different train.

Zones in October 2026

Still three zones. Not a distance fare. Every bus is 1-zone, all day. SkyTrain is 1, 2 or 3 zones on a weekday daytime, and 1-zone after 6:30 p.m. and all weekend and holidays. SeaBus is 1 or 2 zones. Adult stored value: $2.85 / $4.20 / $5.40. Cash, Compass ticket or tap: $3.50 / $5.10 / $6.70. Monthly pass: $117.20 / $156.70 / $211.65. Adult DayPass $12.55, not valid on the West Coast Express. Children 12 and under ride free. A fare lasts 90 minutes, or 120 if the West Coast Express is included.

Compass, briefly

The card is a $6 refundable deposit. Buy it at compasscard.ca, 604-398-2042, station vending machines, Waterfront’s customer service centre, or some London Drugs. Concession cards are not in the machines. Load stored value in amounts of at least $5 — it is the cheaper adult fare. The West Coast Express has its own zones, up to five. Moody Centre, Coquitlam Central and Port Coquitlam are 3 West Coast Express zones from Waterfront. A SkyTrain pass is not a West Coast Express pass. A West Coast Express return or monthly does include bus, SkyTrain and SeaBus that day. Parking was listed at $3 a day or $60 a month.

To downtown, in published minutes

Millennium trains end at VCC–Clark. Waterfront is a transfer, usually at Commercial–Broadway onto the Expo Line. TransLink’s chart: Coquitlam Central to Commercial–Broadway 32 minutes, Lafarge Lake–Douglas to Commercial–Broadway 35, Commercial–Broadway to Waterfront 10. In-vehicle totals: about 42 and 45 minutes, plus the wait for the second train. Peak Expo frequency is published as 2–5 minutes and Millennium as 3–4. West Coast Express, Mission City to Waterfront, is 75 minutes on the weekday timetable (departures 5:25–7:25 a.m., arrivals 6:40–8:40 a.m.). Mornings westbound only. Afternoons eastbound only. Five trains each way. I am not printing driving minutes. The bridges and Highway 1 change by the hour.

Day to dayShopping, parks, schools, care

Shopping. Downtown has street retail and Pacific Centre. Metrotown and Richmond Centre sit on SkyTrain. In the Tri-Cities, Coquitlam Centre is the enclosed mall — about 930,000 square feet, nearly 200 stores, at Lincoln station — with anchors including Walmart, Best Buy, London Drugs, T&T and UNIQLO. Suter Brook Village and Newport Village in Port Moody are outdoor village streets, not malls. Suter Brook’s tenants have included Thrifty Foods. Rocky Point Ice Cream, inside Rocky Point Park, was still open when this was checked in October 2026.

Parks. Stanley Park’s seawall and Kitsilano Beach are the city walks. Buntzen Lake, in Anmore, is a BC Hydro site: parking is free, but recent summers (roughly mid-May to early September) have required a free next-day parking reservation or you are turned away. Walkers, cyclists and the 182 bus do not need one. The 182 stops at Anmore Grocery, about a 30-minute walk to South Beach. Belcarra Regional Park (təmtəmíxʷtən) has Sasamat Lake and the Admiralty Point Trail, about 6 km. Mundy Park is Coquitlam’s 178-hectare urban forest with a 5.5 km loop. Town Centre Park is 42 hectares on Lafarge Lake. Burke Mountain’s city loops are short; Pinecone Burke’s backcountry has poor infrastructure, according to BC Parks.

Schools. The district follows the address. SD39 Vancouver, SD41 Burnaby, SD40 New Westminster, SD38 Richmond, SD44 North Vancouver, SD36 Surrey, SD43 Coquitlam (Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Anmore, Belcarra). Confirm catchment on the district locator. SD43 secondary names on its own 2025–26 list, not a ranking: Centennial, Dr. Charles Best, Gleneagle, Heritage Woods, Pinetree, Port Moody Secondary, Riverside, Terry Fox.

Healthcare. Apply for MSP when you arrive. The wait is the rest of that month plus two months, unless you are a returning Canadian, a permanent resident, arriving under CUEAT, or a discharged CAF family — those waits are waived. Keep other insurance for the gap. Register for a family doctor on the Health Connect Registry. The Province will not estimate the wait. Eagle Ridge Hospital in Port Moody (475 Guildford Way, 604-461-2022) is the Tri-Cities community hospital. Royal Columbian in New Westminster is Fraser Health’s Level 1 trauma centre.

Licence, power, rain. Exchange an out-of-province licence within 90 days at an ICBC office. Bring the licence, ID, and proof of driving history. BC Hydro is electricity; open the account up to 60 days ahead. The first bill includes $13.50 + GST, and some accounts need a deposit. FortisBC is natural gas across the Tri-Cities. Internet: TELUS, Rogers (the former Shaw network), and Novus in some buildings only. Vancouver Harbour normals, 1991–2020: 1,537 mm of precipitation a year, November the wettest month at 245 mm, January average 5.3°C, July 19.0°C. Higher Tri-Cities ground is cooler. I don’t have a local normals table, so I won’t invent one. A waterproof shell beats an umbrella on a SkyTrain platform.

Before you pay anyoneRenting rules

  • Security deposit: at most half a month’s rent. Pet damage deposit, if any, at most another half month.
  • Application fees are illegal under section 15 of the Residential Tenancy Act, including a “credit-check fee.”
  • Rent increases: 2.3% in 2026, 2.2% in 2027, once every 12 months, three full months’ notice on the proper form.
  • Condition inspection (RTB-27) at move-in and move-out. Copy within 7 days of move-in and 15 of move-out.
  • Disputes: Residential Tenancy Branch, filing fee $100, phone 1-800-665-8779.

See the home, in person or on a live video from inside it. Confirm the person can rent it. Be wary of a price far under the asking rents above, of pressure to pay today, and of wire, gift cards or crypto. Most listings appear only a few weeks before move-in. A serious search usually starts 4–6 weeks out.

If you buyProperty transfer tax

On fair market value at registration: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above that, and a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Checked on the Province’s site, last updated 3 December 2025.

Example, not a quote. Coquitlam’s September 2026 apartment benchmark is $640,100. Ordinary tax is $2,000 + 2% of $440,100 = $10,802. A qualifying first-time buyer, on a registration on or after 1 April 2024, gets an $8,000 exemption between $500,000 and $835,000, so tax after the exemption would be $2,802 if every other test is met. Full exemption is at $500,000 or under. It phases out to zero at $860,000. You generally must be a citizen or permanent resident, meet the residence test, never have owned a principal residence anywhere, and live in the home. The lot is generally 0.5 hectares or smaller for the full amount.

A qualifying newly built principal residence can be fully exempt up to $1,100,000. Coquitlam’s townhouse benchmark, $976,600, is under that cap. Ordinary tax on $976,600 is $17,532, which is what the exemption is worth if the home and the buyer both qualify. Foreign buyers can owe an additional 20% on their share of residential property in Metro Vancouver. Exemptions exist. GST on a new home is a separate question I am not calculating here.

  • Sign the condition report.

  • Confirm the school catchment for the signed address.

  • Learn that city’s garbage day.
  • Walk the block in the rain.
  • Renting first is a normal way to learn the region.

UnlistedThings locals know

“The city” is ambiguous. It can mean Vancouver or the whole region.

PoCo is not Coquitlam. Different city, no SkyTrain.

Evergreen is not a transfer. It is the Millennium Line.

West Coast Express is one-way at rush hour. Not a midday SkyTrain.

After 6:30 p.m., long SkyTrain trips are 1-zone. Buy the pass for the daytime trip.

Half a month, not first and last. And no application fee.

The neighbour’s rent is not your rent. CMHC includes sitting tenants. Listings do not.

November is the wet month. 245 mm at Vancouver Harbour, on average.

Buntzen in summer needs a parking pass. Free, and still mandatory in recent seasons.

A listing does not assign a school. The district’s locator does.

MSP does not start on landing day for most newcomers.

Anmore and Belcarra are villages. The nearest rapid transit is in Port Moody.
