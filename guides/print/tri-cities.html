Three cities, two villages

I’m Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate on Clarke Street in Port Moody. “Tri-Cities” means Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam and Port Moody. Anmore and Belcarra are the villages just north.

I’ve lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years and play soccer on a Port Coquitlam team. The five communities show up in the same search; they are not the same errand pattern. The number that matters is your station plus the rent or price on a specific home.

Two rent statistics get mixed up. Asking rents are vacant listings (Rentals.ca, September 2026, published 7 October 2026). CMHC averages include homes people already occupy (October 2025 survey). If you are arriving now, budget the asking rent.

The wider region is in the companion Greater Vancouver guide.

Where to live

Coquitlam and Port Coquitlam

Coquitlam

The largest of the three

From the Fraser up to Burke Mountain and Westwood Plateau. City Centre — Coquitlam Centre, Lafarge Lake, Town Centre Park — is what the SkyTrain was aimed at, and four of the six Evergreen stations are in Coquitlam. The hill is more house and more of a drive. The commute usually decides it.

Names on listings: Burke Mountain, Westwood Plateau, City Centre, Burquitlam, Austin Heights, Maillardville, Ranch Park, Eagle Ridge.

PoCo

Between the Coquitlam and Pitt rivers. A walkable downtown, the Traboulay PoCo Trail, Terry Fox’s hometown, and where I play soccer. Port Moody, Anmore and Belcarra

Port Moody

Head of the inlet

The city calls itself the City of the Arts. Rocky Point, the Shoreline Trail and Brewers Row on Murray Street are local favourites; my office is on Clarke Street. Moody Centre and Inlet Centre have SkyTrain, and Moody Centre has the West Coast Express. The hill neighbourhoods are a drive down to it.

Names on listings: Moody Centre, Inlet Centre and Suter Brook, Newport Village, Heritage Mountain, Heritage Woods, College Park, Glenayre, Ioco.

Anmore & Belcarra

The villages

Larger, treed lots, and a car for most trips. Buntzen Lake is in Anmore. təmtəmíxʷtən/Belcarra Regional Park and Sasamat Lake are why the rest of the region drives up. The nearest SkyTrain and West Coast Express are in Port Moody. Map the grocery trip before you fall for a lot.

Big figures are Compass stored value, the cheaper adult fare. Every bus is 1-zone, all day. SkyTrain is 1, 2 or 3 zones on a weekday daytime, and 1-zone after 6:30 p.m., all weekend and on holidays. Children 12 and under ride free. A fare lasts 90 minutes, or 120 with the West Coast Express.

Compass, briefly

A $6 refundable deposit. Buy it at compasscard.ca, 604-398-2042, station machines, Waterfront’s customer service centre, or some London Drugs. Load at least $5 at a time.

West Coast Express

Its own zones, up to five; Moody Centre, Coquitlam Central and Port Coquitlam are 3 of them from Waterfront. Weekdays only, five trains each way: westbound mornings, eastbound afternoons. Parking was $3 a day or $60 a month. A WCE return or monthly includes bus and SkyTrain that day.

Buses

R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus: Coquitlam Central to Haney Place via Port Coquitlam. 182: the Belcarra bus, via Anmore Grocery for Buntzen. 160: Port Coquitlam Station through Coquitlam Central and Moody Centre to Kootenay Loop. I am not quoting a frequency I could not confirm.

The reason people look here

Lakes and the villages

Anmore

Buntzen Lake

A BC Hydro recreation area. Parking is free. In recent summers, roughly mid-May through early September, you have still needed a free online pass released in the morning for the next day. No pass, no entry by car. Dates change — check bchydro.com/buntzen. No public parking in the village. The 182 bus does not need a pass: about a 30-minute walk from Anmore Grocery to South Beach.

Belcarra

Belcarra Regional Park

Metro Vancouver’s təmtəmíxʷtən/Belcarra Regional Park, at the mouth of the inlet: the beach, Sasamat Lake and White Pine Beach, Woodhaven Swamp, and the Admiralty Point Trail — about 6 km and 1.5 hours over rocky shoreline. On a hot weekend, locals go early. The 182 from Moody Centre is the bus.

May–Sept
Buntzen pass season in recent summers, roughly mid-May to early September. Free, next-day, released in the morning. Walkers, cyclists and the bus do not need one.
30 min

Shopping and schools

Coquitlam Centre

At Lincoln station: about 930,000 square feet and nearly 200 stores. The mall’s own list includes Walmart, Best Buy, London Drugs, Sport Chek, T&T, UNIQLO, H&M, Sephora and lululemon, with about 4,000 parking stalls.

Village streets

Suter Brook Village (near Inlet Centre) and Newport Village (across from Port Moody City Hall and the library) are mixed-use streets, not malls. Suter Brook has included Thrifty Foods. Brewers Row on Murray Street and Rocky Point Ice Cream are local favourites, not a ranking. Port Coquitlam’s downtown is the smaller main street.

Schools: SD43

School District No. 43 (Coquitlam) serves all five communities. Catchment follows the permanent address, and boundaries change — use the district’s School Locator once you have an address, not a sentence in a listing. Secondary schools on the district’s own 2025–26 contact list, as examples only: Centennial, Dr. Charles Best, Gleneagle and Pinetree in Coquitlam; Heritage Woods and Port Moody Secondary in Port Moody; Riverside and Terry Fox in Port Coquitlam. Burnaby is SD41 and Vancouver is SD39: a city boundary is a district boundary.

Day to day

Healthcare and the setup

Healthcare

Apply for MSP when you arrive. The wait is the rest of that month plus two months, unless you are a returning Canadian, a permanent resident, arriving under CUEAT, or a discharged CAF family — those waits are waived. Keep other insurance for the gap. Register for a family doctor on the Health Connect Registry; the Province will not estimate the wait. Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, 604-461-2022, is the local emergency department. Royal Columbian in New Westminster is Fraser Health’s Level 1 trauma centre. Know which is which.

Licence, power, gas, internet

Exchange an out-of-province licence within 90 days at an ICBC office; bring the licence, ID and proof of driving history. BC Hydro is electricity: open the account up to 60 days ahead, and the first bill includes $13.50 + GST. FortisBC is natural gas in Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody and Anmore — confirm the address has it. Internet: TELUS PureFibre, Rogers on the former Shaw network, and Novus in some buildings only.

Weather

Vancouver Harbour averages 1,537 mm of precipitation a year and 245 mm in November. Burke Mountain, Heritage Mountain and the villages are higher and see more snow than Clarke Street. I am not quoting a Port Moody climate table I do not have.

Before you pay anyone · Residential Tenancy Act, checked 11 October 2026

Renting rules

½ monthSecurity deposit

At most half a month’s rent. Pet damage deposit, if any, at most another half month. Not first-and-last.

$0Application fee

Application fees are illegal under section 15 of the Residential Tenancy Act, including a “credit-check fee.”

2.3%2026 increase cap

2.2% in 2027. Once every 12 months, with three full months’ notice on the proper form.

7 / 15days · RTB-27

Condition inspection at move-in and move-out. You get a copy within 7 days of move-in and 15 of move-out. That is the deposit fight, if there is one.

$100Dispute filing

Residential Tenancy Branch filing fee. Phone 1‑800‑665‑8779.

4–6weeks out

Listings usually appear a few weeks before they are free. Plan the active search for about 4–6 weeks before you need keys.

Before you send money

See the suite yourself, or on a live video from inside it.

A price far under Coquitlam’s $2,497 asking average (page ), plus pressure to pay today by wire, gift card or crypto, is the pattern. Confirm the person can rent it. Half a month is the legal deposit ceiling, and nobody may charge you to apply.

If you buy · Province of B.C., page last updated 3 December 2025

Property transfer tax

Charged on fair market value at registration. The rate steps up through the price; each band is taxed at its own rate.

1%on the first $200,000
2%from $200,000 to $2,000,000
3%above $2,000,000
+2%further, on the residential portion above $3,000,000

Worked examples, not quotes

$1,000

Newly built · Coquitlam townhouse
$17,532
Ordinary tax on $976,600. A qualifying newly built principal residence can be fully exempt up to $1,100,000, so that is what the exemption is worth.

First-time buyers’ exemption

Full exemption at $500,000 or under; it phases out to zero at $860,000. Port Moody’s apartment benchmark ($676,200) is in the band; its detached benchmark ($1,903,500) is not. You generally must be a citizen or permanent resident, meet the residence test, never have owned a principal residence anywhere, and live in the home.

Two more lines

Foreign buyers can owe an additional 20% on their share of residential property in Metro Vancouver; exemptions exist. GST on many new homes is a separate question I am not calculating here. For the first-time exemption the lot is generally 0.5 hectares or smaller for the full amount — relevant in Anmore. Not tax advice.

Things locals know

Three cities, two villages. Different city halls. Anmore is not north Port Moody.

PoCo has no SkyTrain. “The train” may mean a service that does not run at noon.

You don’t transfer to Evergreen. You stay on the Millennium Line.

Lincoln is the mall. Lafarge Lake is the park. One stop apart.

Inlet Centre and Moody Centre are both Port Moody. Not the same walk: Suter Brook vs. Clarke Street.

The hill is a lifestyle. Heritage Mountain, Westwood Plateau and Burke Mountain are a drive down to the train.

Buntzen’s pass is the whole plan on a summer Saturday. Free, and still required in recent seasons.

Half a month’s rent is the legal deposit ceiling. And no application fee.

CMHC’s $1,692 Coquitlam 1‑bedroom is not the rent on a listing this month.

SD43 does not follow a sentence in a listing. The locator follows the address.

Eagle Ridge is the local ER. Royal Columbian is the trauma centre, in New Westminster.

November is a season. The mountain gets more weather than Clarke Street.
