FAQ

About the rent opinion

Is this an appraisal?

No. It's my opinion of what the home could rent for, based on comparable homes currently asking rent nearby. It isn't an appraisal, and it isn't a promise of what a tenant will pay.

Does asking cost anything?

No. Asking me what your home could rent for has no charge, and it doesn't commit you to anything.

Will you manage the rental for me?

No. I don't manage rental properties. If you want the home managed, I can connect you with Axford Property Management, my brokerage's licensed property management team. Any management agreement, including any management fee, is between you and Axford Property Management.

What happens to the details I send?

The form emails your details to me through FormSubmit, and I use them to reply about your request. The privacy page explains how this site's forms handle what you send.
