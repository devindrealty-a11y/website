If you are moving from Ontario

Start with the rules that actually change

Where you are actually moving

People often say “Vancouver” when they mean the wider region. Greater Vancouver includes the City of Vancouver plus Burnaby, New Westminster, Richmond, the North Shore, Surrey, Langley, and the Tri-Cities, BC: Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam and Port Moody, along with Anmore and Belcarra. I would start with where you will work or study, then shortlist two or three areas.

Tenancy law

Who handles a dispute, and what a deposit is

Ontario · Landlord and Tenant Board

Residential Tenancies Act, 2006

  • Most residential tenancies go through the Landlord and Tenant Board.
  • A landlord may require a rent deposit on or before the agreement. For a monthly tenancy it cannot be more than one month’s rent.
  • That deposit is applied to the last rent period. It is the only security deposit the Act allows. It is not a damage deposit.
  • The landlord pays interest on it each year, at the rent increase guideline then in effect.

Residential Tenancies Act, 2006, sections 105 and 106. ontario.ca/laws/statute/06r17

British Columbia · Residential Tenancy Branch

Residential Tenancy Act

  • The Residential Tenancy Branch gives information and dispute resolution for most residential tenancies.
  • A security deposit can be no more than half of the first month’s rent. The amount stays tied to the original rent, even if rent changes later.
  • If pets are allowed, a pet damage deposit can be another half month, no matter how many pets. Guide and service dogs are not pets, and there is no monthly pet fee.
  • Together, the two deposits can equal one month’s rent. A landlord cannot charge a fee to accept, review or process an application.

Province of British Columbia, “Tenancy deposits and fees,” updated March 4, 2026.

The Province’s own example: if the monthly rent is $1,500, the security deposit cannot be more than $750. That $1,500 is the Province’s example, not a statement of Greater Vancouver rent. Using that same example so the two caps can be compared, Ontario’s rent deposit could be up to $1,500 and would have to be applied to the last rent period. B.C.’s security deposit would be at most $750, plus at most $750 more if pets are allowed.

After you pay in Ontario

  • The deposit comes off the last rent period, not off damage.
  • If the landlord does not pay the yearly interest when it is due, you may deduct it from a later rent payment.

After you pay in B.C.

  • Once the security deposit is paid, the tenancy is established. The landlord cannot then rent the unit to someone else.
  • You cannot put a deposit toward rent unless the landlord agrees in writing.
  • You and the landlord complete a Condition Inspection Report, Form RTB-27. A landlord can keep a deposit only with your written permission or an RTB order.
  • After you give a forwarding address in writing, the landlord has 15 days to return the deposit with any interest if there is no dispute. The interest rate for 2026 is 0%.
How to get ahead of it. Budget half a month, not a full extra month the way Ontario’s last-period deposit works. If you have a pet, budget a second half month. Do not pay an application fee, and do not pay for the only key: B.C. says a landlord cannot charge for the only means of entry. Get the agreement in writing before money moves, keep the receipt, and do the move-in inspection. Pay the security deposit within 30 days of signing, or the landlord can serve notice to end the tenancy.

Rent increases and the lease

The yearly cap is not the rent you agree on day one

Ontario guideline

2.1% in 2026

1.9% in 2027

  • For most tenants, rent can rise by no more than the guideline without Landlord and Tenant Board approval.
  • At least 12 months since the tenancy began or the last increase, and at least 90 days’ written notice on the Board’s form.
  • The guideline is capped at 2.5%. It does not apply to units first occupied for residential use after November 15, 2018, or to a new rent agreed when a tenancy turns over.
  • Ontario’s own sample: 1.9% on $1,000 of rent in 2027 is $19, to $1,019.

ontario.ca, “Residential rent increases,” read November 20, 2026.

B.C. allowable increase

2.3% in 2026

2.2% in 2027

  • For an existing residential tenancy, a landlord can increase rent once every 12 months, and only within that year’s limit. They cannot round up.
  • You need at least three full months’ written notice on Form RTB-7, with the dollar amount and the date.
  • The 2027 limit takes effect January 1, 2027. A landlord cannot add an unused portion of last year’s increase to this year’s cap.
  • The cap limits an increase during a tenancy. The rent at the start is the rent in the agreement.

Province of British Columbia, “Rent increases,” read November 20, 2026. Some tenancies, including rent geared to income, are outside this limit. The page lists them.

How to get ahead of it. When you are still in Ontario, do not compare a B.C. asking rent with an Ontario renewal cap. The cap applies after you are in the home. Read the notice when it eventually arrives: in B.C. it has to be Form RTB-7, three full months ahead, and inside that year’s percentage. The Residential Tenancy Branch has a rent increase calculator.

Ontario standard lease

Required for most new private leases

The standard lease is required for most residential tenancy agreements signed on or after April 30, 2018. It sets out the total lawful rent, what is included, and the rules about the unit. It does not apply to care homes, most social housing, co-ops, and some other tenancies.

If you were entitled to one and did not get it, you can ask in writing. The landlord then has 21 days. Ontario’s renting-rights page explains the withholding rules if it still does not arrive.

ontario.ca, “Guide to Ontario’s standard lease,” and “Renting in Ontario: Your rights.”

B.C. written agreement

Form RTB-1 carries the standard terms

A landlord must prepare a written agreement for every tenancy. Both of you sign and date it. The landlord can use their own form, but it has to include every standard term. Residential Tenancy Agreement Form RTB-1 includes those terms.

The agreement names the people and the unit, the start date, whether it is month-to-month or fixed term, the rent and what it covers, and any deposits. A fixed term does not usually end just because the end date arrives, unless a narrow vacate clause applies. The landlord must give you a copy within 21 days.

Province of British Columbia, “Tenancy agreements,” updated March 4, 2026.

How to get ahead of it. Ask for the written agreement before you send a deposit. Check the rent, the date, what utilities are included, pets, and any extra term such as tenant insurance. Photograph the pages the day you sign. A term that contradicts the Residential Tenancy Act cannot be enforced. I can go through a lease with you. I am not a lawyer, and that read is not legal advice.

Health, licence, transit, deposits

Four clocks that start when you arrive

MSPRest of the arrival month, plus two months
ICBC90 days for the licence, 30 days for a vehicle
Compass$6 refundable card. Tap in and out.
DepositsHalf a month, plus half more if you have pets

OHIP after you leave

Through the second full month

If you are moving permanently to another province, OHIP covers the same services as a temporary absence until the last day of the second full month after you leave Ontario. Apply for coverage in the new province.

In another province, OHIP does not cover ambulance services, or prescription drugs given outside a hospital. Ontario recommends private insurance for uninsured services.

ontario.ca, “OHIP coverage outside Ontario,” updated May 2, 2024.

MSP wait period

Then two more months

New residents complete a wait: the rest of the month you become a B.C. resident, plus two more months. The Ministry of Health sets the date you become a resident. Apply as soon as you arrive, not at the end of the wait. Each family member has their own wait, based on that person’s arrival.

If you move from another part of Canada, keep your former health plan during the wait. You can get needed care before MSP starts, and you pay the full cost unless other insurance covers it.

Province of British Columbia, MSP coverage wait period, updated June 4, 2026. Questions: Health Insurance BC, 604-683-7151 in the Lower Mainland.

How to get ahead of it. Put “apply for MSP” on arrival week, and keep your Ontario health card in your wallet until MSP starts. If you want cover for an ambulance or a prescription during the gap, arrange that insurance before you leave. I am not quoting a premium.

Your Ontario licence and policy

They do not become a B.C. policy

You can drive on your valid Ontario licence while you are inside the 90 days. When you qualify for the B.C. licence, you surrender the Ontario one. B.C. law allows only one driver’s licence.

An Ontario auto policy does not replace B.C. registration. If you bring a vehicle, basic coverage here is ICBC Basic Autoplan, and it is mandatory.

ICBC, from within Canada

90 days, and 30 for the car

Book a driver licensing office. Bring accepted ID, your current licence, the licence fee, and proof of driving experience. Proof of insurance is no longer required for the licence. Premiums are adjusted for the first three years. ICBC credits up to 15 years of non-learner experience from a driver’s abstract.

You have 30 days to register, licence and insure a vehicle. Commercial vehicles must be done immediately. If you cannot prove two years of full-privilege, non-learner experience, you go through graduated licensing, with credit for the time you have driven. Emailed records are not accepted for that exemption. Bring an original abstract or an original licence that shows the issue date.

ICBC, “Moving from within Canada,” “Proving your driving experience,” “Moving to, from or within B.C.,” and “Basic insurance.” I am not printing a licence fee or an insurance premium. Those amounts are set at the office from your record, and I could not confirm one new-resident licence price on an ICBC fee page.

How to get ahead of it. Order an original Ontario driver’s abstract before you fly, and book the ICBC appointment for the first weeks you are here. The 90 days go quickly if the office is booked. If you are shipping a car, start the 30-day registration clock on arrival day, not when the car is unpacked.

From the first search to the first month

A timeline, then the first 30 days

The dates for MSP, OHIP and ICBC come from the government. The search window below is from my work with people moving here. It is not a government rule, and it is not a promise that a rental will be ready by a set date.

  1. Before you search
    Save these as PDFs: a clear, unexpired government photo ID; proof of income (recent pay stubs, an offer letter, or a contract; if you are self-employed, bank statements or a Notice of Assessment); landlord and employer references with a phone or email; and a short note on who will live there, the move-in date, pets and must-haves. A credit report you pull yourself is optional. Your Canadian credit file can travel with you; a landlord may still ask for references and proof of income.
  2. About 6 weeks out
    Set the budget using the table on page 6. Do not stack an Ontario-style last month with a separate damage deposit. B.C. does not work that way. Pick two or three areas: for many people that is the Tri-Cities, BC, plus Burnaby or Vancouver. Check the commute on TransLink at the time you would actually travel.
  3. 4 to 6 weeks out
    Most Greater Vancouver rentals are listed only a few weeks before they are available. This is when I usually ask people to start the active search. Book a showing the day a fit appears. If you are still in Ontario, ask for a live video walkthrough and a floor plan rather than relying on photos.
  4. Offer week
    Confirm the unit, and confirm who you are dealing with, before any money moves. Sign a written tenancy agreement. Pay only the deposits B.C. allows. Keep the receipt.
  5. Move-in day
    Walk the unit and complete Form RTB-27 with the landlord. Photograph the condition. Collect the keys. Open BC Hydro if electricity will be in your name, before the lights go off. Ask what else is in your name: heat, hot water, internet, parking.
  6. First 30 days
    The checklist below. The ICBC vehicle clock is 30 days. The licence clock is 90 days. MSP should be applied for in week one, even though coverage starts later.
How to get ahead of the search. Have the PDF pack ready so you can apply the same day. Do not send a deposit for a home nobody you trust has seen. A landlord who will not show the unit, will not put the agreement in writing, or asks for more than half a month plus a pet deposit is outside the rules on page 3.

First 30 days

  • Apply for MSP in the first week. Keep OHIP through the wait.
  • Get a Compass Card ($6 deposit) and ride your real commute once.
  • Open BC Hydro before or on move-in day ($13.50 plus GST).
  • Book ICBC. Licence within 90 days. Vehicle within 30 days.
  • Bring the original driving record, not only an email.
  • Store the signed agreement, deposit receipt and RTB-27 together.
  • If children are in school, register with the local district once you have an address. School District 43 covers Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Anmore and Belcarra.
  • Walk one nearby park. Lafarge Lake in Coquitlam and Rocky Point in Port Moody are on the cover and on page 6.

School District 43 registration page: sd43.bc.ca. The district’s own page is the one to follow for documents. I am not summarizing eligibility beyond the cities it serves.
