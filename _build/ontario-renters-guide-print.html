If you are moving from Ontario

The rules change when you cross into B.C.

I am Devin Desaulniers, a Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate in Port Moody. I have lived in Greater Vancouver for 28 years, and I have been licensed in British Columbia since 2020. I wrote this for people who will rent here after a move from Ontario.

The place

Greater Vancouver is a set of cities.

People say “Vancouver” when they mean the wider region: the City of Vancouver, Burnaby, the North Shore, and the Tri-Cities, BC, which are Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam and Port Moody, along with Anmore and Belcarra. Start with where you will work or study, then shortlist two or three areas.

Who handles a deposit

Ontario’s board, or B.C.’s branch.

Ontario

Landlord and Tenant Board

1 month

The most a rent deposit can be, on a monthly tenancy. It is applied to the last rent period.

  • The Residential Tenancies Act, 2006, sections 105 and 106.
  • It is the only security deposit the Act allows. It is not a damage deposit.
  • The landlord pays interest on it each year, at that year’s rent-increase guideline.

British Columbia

Residential Tenancy Branch

½ month

The most a security deposit can be. A pet damage deposit can be another half month.

  • Province of B.C., “Tenancy deposits and fees,” updated March 4, 2026.
  • Any number of pets. A certified guide or service dog is not a pet, and there is no monthly pet fee.
  • No fee to accept, review or process an application. The two deposits together can equal one month.
$1,500The Province’s own rent example. Not a Greater Vancouver rent.
$1,500Ontario’s cap on that example, applied to the last rent period.
$750B.C.’s security-deposit cap on that same example. Pets: up to $750 more.

After the money moves

In B.C. the deposit is not your last month.

After you pay in Ontario

Last period

  • The deposit comes off the last rent period, not off damage.
  • If the yearly interest is not paid when it is due, you may deduct it from a later rent payment.

Residential Tenancies Act, 2006, s. 106.

After you pay in B.C.

15 days

  • Once the security deposit is paid, the tenancy is established. The landlord cannot then rent the unit to someone else.
  • You cannot put a deposit toward rent unless the landlord agrees in writing.
  • A landlord keeps a deposit only with your written permission or an RTB order.

Province of B.C., deposits page.

Form RTB-27You and the landlord complete a Condition Inspection Report at move-in and move-out. That report is what a later deposit claim is compared with.
0% in 2026After you give a forwarding address in writing, the landlord has 15 days to return the deposit with any interest, if there is no dispute. The interest rate for 2026 is 0%.
30 days to payPay the security deposit within 30 days of signing. If you do not, the landlord can serve notice to end the tenancy.
The only keyA landlord cannot charge you for the only key or access device.
How to get ahead of it. Budget half a month, not a full extra month the way Ontario’s last-period deposit works. If you have a pet, budget a second half month. Do not pay an application fee. Get the agreement in writing before money moves, and keep the receipt.

Rent increases

The yearly cap is not the rent you agree on day one.

Ontario guideline

2.1%

2026. The 2027 guideline is 1.9%.

  • For most tenants, no more than the guideline without Landlord and Tenant Board approval.
  • At least 12 months since the start or the last increase, and 90 days’ written notice on the Board’s form.
  • The guideline is capped at 2.5%. It does not apply to units first occupied after November 15, 2018, or to a new rent when a tenancy turns over.
  • Ontario’s sample: 1.9% on $1,000 in 2027 is $19.

ontario.ca, “Residential rent increases.”

B.C. allowable increase

2.3%

2026. The 2027 limit is 2.2%, from January 1, 2027.

  • Once every 12 months, inside that year’s limit. A landlord cannot round up.
  • Three full months’ written notice on Form RTB-7, with the dollar amount and the date.
  • An unused piece of last year’s increase cannot be added to this year’s cap.
  • The cap limits an increase during a tenancy. The starting rent is the rent in the agreement.

Province of B.C., “Rent increases.” Some tenancies, including rent geared to income, sit outside this limit.

How to get ahead of it. Do not compare a B.C. asking rent with an Ontario renewal cap. The cap applies after you are in the home. When a notice arrives, check that it is Form RTB-7, three full months ahead, and inside that year’s percentage. The Residential Tenancy Branch has a calculator.

The agreement

Get it in writing before you send a deposit.

Ontario standard lease

Required for most new private leases

The standard lease is required for most residential agreements signed on or after April 30, 2018. It sets the total lawful rent and what is included. It does not apply to care homes, most social housing, co-ops, and some other tenancies.

If you were entitled to one and did not get it, ask in writing. The landlord then has 21 days. Ontario’s renting-rights page explains what happens if it still does not arrive.

ontario.ca, standard lease guide, and “Renting in Ontario: Your rights.”

B.C. written agreement

Form RTB-1 carries the standard terms

A landlord must prepare a written agreement for every tenancy. Both of you sign and date it. Their own form is allowed only if it includes every standard term. Form RTB-1 includes those terms.

It names the people and the unit, the start, whether it is month-to-month or a fixed term, the rent, and any deposits. A fixed term does not usually end just because the end date arrives. You must receive a copy within 21 days.

Province of B.C., “Tenancy agreements,” updated March 4, 2026.

How to get ahead of it. Read the rent, the date, what utilities are included, pets, and any extra term such as tenant insurance. Photograph the pages the day you sign. A term that contradicts the Residential Tenancy Act cannot be enforced. I can go through a lease with you. I am not a lawyer, and that read is not legal advice.

Health coverage

OHIP carries you. MSP does not start on arrival day.

MSPRest of the month, plus two
ICBC90 days, and 30 for a car
Compass$6 card. Tap in and out.
DepositsHalf a month, plus half for pets

OHIP after you leave

2nd full month

If you move permanently to another province, OHIP covers the same services as a temporary absence until the last day of the second full month after you leave Ontario. Apply in the new province.

In another province, OHIP does not cover ambulance services, or prescription drugs given outside a hospital. Ontario recommends private insurance for uninsured services.

ontario.ca, “OHIP coverage outside Ontario,” updated May 2, 2024.

MSP wait

+ 2 months

The wait is the rest of the month you become a B.C. resident, plus two more months. The Ministry of Health sets that date. Apply as soon as you arrive. Each family member has their own wait.

Keep your former provincial plan during the wait. You can get needed care before MSP starts, and you pay the full cost unless other insurance covers it.

MSP wait-period page, updated June 4, 2026. Health Insurance BC: 604-683-7151 in the Lower Mainland.

How to get ahead of it. Put “apply for MSP” on arrival week, and keep your Ontario health card until MSP starts. If you want cover for an ambulance or a prescription during the gap, arrange that insurance before you leave. I am not quoting a premium.

Your licence and your car

An Ontario policy does not become a B.C. policy.

While the Ontario licence is still valid

90 days

You can drive on it during that window. When you qualify for the B.C. licence, you surrender the Ontario one. B.C. allows only one driver’s licence.

An Ontario auto policy does not replace B.C. registration. If you bring a vehicle, basic coverage here is ICBC Basic Autoplan, and it is mandatory.

What to take to ICBC

30 days

To register, licence and insure a vehicle. Commercial vehicles must be done immediately.

  • Accepted ID, your current licence, the licence fee, and proof of driving experience.
  • Proof of insurance is no longer required for the licence. Premiums are adjusted for the first three years.
  • ICBC credits up to 15 years of non-learner experience. Emailed records are not accepted for the graduated-licensing exemption. Bring an original abstract, or an original licence that shows the issue date.
How to get ahead of it. Order an original Ontario driver’s abstract before you fly, and book the ICBC appointment for the first weeks you are here. If you are shipping a car, start the 30-day clock on arrival day. I am not printing a licence fee or an insurance premium. I could not confirm one new-resident licence price on an ICBC fee page, and the premium depends on your record.

ICBC: “Moving from within Canada,” “Proving your driving experience,” “Moving to, from or within B.C.,” and “Basic insurance.”

From the first search to the keys

A timeline you can actually follow.

MSP, OHIP and ICBC dates come from the government. The search window is from my work with people moving here. It is not a government rule, and it is not a promise that a rental will be ready by a set date.

  1. Before you searchPDFs ready: photo ID, proof of income, landlord and employer references, and a short note on who will live there, the date, pets and must-haves. A credit report you pull yourself is optional. Your Canadian credit file can travel with you.
  2. About 6 weeksSet the budget from pages 11 and 12. Do not stack an Ontario-style last month with a separate damage deposit. Shortlist two or three areas, often the Tri-Cities, BC plus Burnaby or Vancouver.
  3. 4 to 6 weeksMost rentals are listed only a few weeks before they are available. This is when I ask people to start. If you are still in Ontario, ask for a live video walkthrough and a floor plan.
  4. Offer weekConfirm the unit and who you are dealing with before any money moves. Sign a written agreement. Pay only the deposits B.C. allows.
  5. Move-in dayComplete Form RTB-27. Photograph the condition. Collect the keys. Open BC Hydro if electricity will be in your name.
  6. First 30 daysThe checklist on the next page. Apply for MSP in week one. The vehicle clock is 30 days. The licence clock is 90 days.
How to get ahead of the search. Apply the same day a fit appears. Do not send a deposit for a home nobody you trust has seen. A person who will not show the unit, will not put the agreement in writing, or asks for more than the deposits on page 5 is outside those rules.

First 30 days

The list I would tape inside a cupboard.

  • Apply for MSP in the first week. Keep OHIP through the wait.
  • Get a Compass Card, the $6 deposit, and ride your real commute once.
  • Open BC Hydro before or on move-in day. The account charge is $13.50 plus GST.
  • Book ICBC. Licence within 90 days. Vehicle within 30 days.
  • Bring the original driving record, not only an email.
  • Keep the signed agreement, the deposit receipt and Form RTB-27 together.
  • If children are in school, register with the local district once you have an address. School District 43 covers Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Anmore and Belcarra.
  • Walk somewhere nearby. Lafarge Lake in Coquitlam and Rocky Point in Port Moody are in this guide.

School District 43: sd43.bc.ca. Follow the district’s own page for documents. I am not summarizing eligibility beyond the cities it serves.
