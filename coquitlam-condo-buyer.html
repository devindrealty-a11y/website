Three clusters

Burquitlam, City Centre and Northeast Coquitlam

Condo buyers in Coquitlam are usually choosing among these three, and the commute decides it more often than the finish package.

Burquitlam

Nearest SkyTrain
Burquitlam Station, Millennium Line
Typical buildings
Condos and townhomes, with high-rises close to the station and along North Road.
Getting around
SkyTrain toward Burnaby and Vancouver. Lougheed Town Centre, one stop west, connects to the Expo Line.
Watch for
Older streets near the station can be designated for future towers under the Burquitlam-Lougheed plan and the provincial transit-oriented rules. Check the City's land-use map for the address. The Burquitlam guide has the planning detail.

City Centre

Nearest SkyTrain
Lincoln and Lafarge Lake–Douglas. Coquitlam Central, between them, is also a West Coast Express stop.
Typical buildings
High-rise condos near the mall, Town Centre Park and Douglas College.
Getting around
Walkable to SkyTrain. A reasonable choice if the commute into Burnaby or Vancouver is the point.
Watch for
Strata fees on a tower are specific to that building. Read them on the Form B. Don't assume a neighbouring tower charges the same.

Northeast Coquitlam

Nearest SkyTrain
No SkyTrain station on Burke Mountain.
Typical buildings
Newer subdivisions and townhomes, with some apartments. More space than the station areas.
Getting around
Mostly driving or a bus. Plan the commute before you fall for the view.
Watch for
The trade-off is the drive. Burke Mountain is the northeast side I describe on the Coquitlam guide: newer, and car-friendly.
Before subject removal

Strata documents I read with you

A condo is a share of a building, not just a suite. These are the eight things I want in hand before you remove subjects. Your notary or lawyer reviews them for the legal side. I read them for what they mean for the price and the building.

  • Form BMonthly strata fees, special levies, and the attached budget, rules and latest depreciation report.
  • Depreciation reportWhat the building expects to repair, and roughly when.
  • Minutes, last two yearsCouncil, AGM and special general meeting minutes. This is where leaks, disputes and special levies show up.
  • Bylaws and rulesPets, rentals, renovations, barbecues, smoking. A bylaw can matter more than the floor plan.
  • Budget and financial statementsWhether the fees cover the work the building is actually doing.
  • Contingency reserve fundThe balance set against the work in the depreciation report, not the balance on its own.
  • Insurance summary and deductibleWhat the strata policy covers, and how large a deductible an owner could be asked to pay.
  • Parking and lockerWhich stall and locker are assigned to this strata lot, on the Form B, not just in the listing remarks.
Email me this checklist

The Form B is described by the Province of British Columbia. What a particular building's minutes say is specific to that building.

FAQ

Coquitlam condo questions

Who should I hire to buy a condo in Coquitlam?

Hire a B.C.-licensed buyer's agent who represents you, and who will read the Form B, the minutes and the depreciation report with you before you remove subjects. I'm Devin Desaulniers, a REALTOR® and Real Estate Associate with Axford Real Estate in Port Moody, licensed since 2020. I do that work in Burquitlam, City Centre and Northeast Coquitlam, and across the Tri-Cities, BC.

What should I check before I remove subjects on a Coquitlam condo?

The strata file, not just the suite. Start with the Form B, the depreciation report, the last two years of council, AGM and special general meeting minutes, the bylaws and rules, the budget and financial statements, the contingency reserve fund set against upcoming work, the insurance summary and deductible, and which parking stall and locker are assigned to the strata lot. I go through that list with you and flag questions for your notary, lawyer or inspector.

How do Burquitlam, City Centre and Northeast Coquitlam differ for a condo?

Burquitlam is condos and townhomes around Burquitlam Station, with Millennium Line access and ongoing redevelopment near the station. City Centre is high-rise living near Lincoln and Lafarge Lake–Douglas, the mall, Town Centre Park and Douglas College, so compare strata fees on the Form B rather than assuming two towers match. Northeast Coquitlam, including Burke Mountain, is newer and more car-friendly: there is no SkyTrain station up there. The Burquitlam guide and the Coquitlam guide go further on each area.

What did Coquitlam apartments benchmark at in October 2026?

The MLS® Home Price Index benchmark for a Coquitlam apartment was $624,200 in October 2026, down 9.1% from a year earlier and 2.5% from September, in the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® statistics package. That is a typical apartment, not the middle sale. The median Coquitlam apartment sale that month was $595,000, from 82 sales. The Metro Vancouver apartment benchmark was $673,400.

What is a Form B?

The Form B Information Certificate is a strata document ordered on a sale. It shows the monthly strata fee and any special levies, and it attaches the budget, the rules and the most recent depreciation report. It is one piece of the file, not the whole file. The province describes it on the Form B page.

Can I buy a Coquitlam condo if I haven't visited yet?

Yes. I can walk the unit and the street with you on a live video call, and most of the paperwork can be signed electronically. The steps for buying from another province are on the Coquitlam guide. A lot of buyers still like one short trip before they firm up an offer.

Who pays the buyer's agent on a Coquitlam condo?

In most B.C. sales the buyer's agent is paid from the commission the seller has already agreed to with the listing brokerage, so many buyers pay nothing directly. That isn't guaranteed. The agreement we sign says what you would owe if the listing brokerage doesn't pay the amount we agreed, and I'll explain that in writing before you sign anything. The longer version is on the buyers page.
